LoungeOS in Africa

A restaurant POS built in Africa, for African conditions

Imported POS systems are designed for reliable broadband and card-first customers. LoungeOS is designed for what African restaurants, bars and lounges actually face: outages, mobile money and cash that needs watching.

LoungeOS is an offline-first point of sale and management system developed in Cameroon and used by restaurants, bars, lounges, nightclubs, cafés and hotels across Africa. It runs on a Windows computer inside the venue, connects staff phones over Wi-Fi and does not need internet during service.

Three things an African restaurant POS must do

1. Keep working through outages

Power cuts and internet outages are routine in many African cities. A cloud POS that depends on a connection either stops or switches to a limited offline mode with sync problems. LoungeOS runs on your local network, so service continues as long as the host computer and router have power from a UPS, inverter or laptop battery. See offline POS.

2. Handle mobile money properly

Mobile money is how many customers pay. LoungeOS records MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money payments with their transaction reference, alongside cash and card, and splits one bill across several methods. That reference is what lets you catch fake confirmations at closing. See mobile money payments.

3. Stop cash and stock leaks

Cash-heavy businesses lose money through deleted items, unauthorised discounts and missing bottles. LoungeOS gives each staff member a role and a login, requires a manager for sensitive changes and a reason for every cancellation, and logs everything. See loss prevention.

LoungeOS also works in Gabon, Congo, Chad, the Central African Republic and Equatorial Guinea (FCFA zone, built-in XAF), and in any other country where you can run a Windows computer. Add your currency as a custom currency and configure your local taxes.

A note on e-invoicing and fiscal devices

Several African tax authorities now require electronic invoicing or certified fiscal devices for some or all businesses. Examples include Kenya’s eTIMS, Rwanda’s EBM and Tanzania’s EFD. LoungeOS does not currently connect to these systems. If your country requires certified invoicing, check with your tax adviser how it fits alongside your POS before you subscribe. Tell us at hello@loungeos.app which integration you need, because country demand decides what we build next.

Frequently asked questions

Which African countries can use LoungeOS?

Any country. LoungeOS runs on a Windows computer on your own network, so it does not depend on local cloud availability. Currencies not built in can be added as custom currencies.

Is LoungeOS available in French?

Yes. The interface is available in French and English, and our French-language pages for francophone Africa start at loungeos.app/fr.

Does LoungeOS work with M-Pesa, Wave or Airtel Money?

The built-in mobile money options are MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money. Payments from other wallets can be recorded under card or bank transfer. Tell us which wallets you need.
