The best POS systems in Cameroon in 2026

A POS that shines in Paris or Texas can fall over on a Saturday night in Douala when ENEO cuts the power and the internet follows. We compared the options Cameroonian owners actually choose between, on the things that matter here.

By LoungeOS TeamPublished November 12, 2026Updated November 12, 20265 min read

Key takeaways

  • In Cameroon the deciding questions are: does it keep selling during cuts, does it record MTN MoMo and Orange Money with references, can you pay for it in FCFA, and is there support in French and English on WhatsApp?
  • LoungeOS (made in Cameroon) fits bars, lounges, restaurants and shops that need offline tills, staff control and FCFA billing.
  • Loyverse is the best free start for a single counter with stable internet. Odoo POS suits businesses that want a full ERP and have a local integrator. SambaPOS suits venues with technical support.
  • Card-first systems such as Square and Toast are not offered in Cameroon.

Most “best POS” lists are written for the United States or Europe. They rank systems on card processing, delivery apps and payroll integrations. In Cameroon, the questions are different, and a wrong choice shows up on the first busy night with a power cut.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS, in Cameroon. We include the other systems Cameroonian owners ask us about, with their strengths, and link to their own documentation so you can check.

What matters in Cameroon

  1. Internet and power cuts. Does the till keep taking orders, sending them to the kitchen, taking payments and printing receipts with no internet? For how long?
  2. Mobile money. Can you record MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money as separate methods, with the transaction reference, and pay one bill with two methods?
  3. Paying for it. Is the price in FCFA, payable by MoMo or OM, or only by international card in dollars or euros?
  4. French and English. For the screens, receipts and reports, not only the website.
  5. Staff control. Cancellations, discounts and refunds approved by a manager, with a log. In bars this is where the money goes.
  6. Support you can reach. A WhatsApp number in Cameroon beats a ticket form in another time zone.
  7. Hardware you already have. A laptop, Android phones, a 58 or 80 mm thermal printer.

1. LoungeOS: built for Cameroonian venues

LoungeOS is point of sale and management software developed in Cameroon by Sigalix. It runs in the web app, on Windows and Mac tills that keep selling without internet, and on Android phones.

  • Through cuts: the Windows and Mac tills keep every function (orders, kitchen and bar screens, payments, refunds, customer tabs, stock) and sync when the connection is back. Staff phones can keep ordering over the venue’s Wi-Fi.
  • Money: Cash, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money and card ready, each able to require the transaction reference; two methods on one bill; customer tabs for credit.
  • Control: manager PIN for cancellations, refunds and price changes, and an activity log.
  • Price: 6,000, 15,000 or 30,000 FCFA a month, 14-day free trial, paid by mobile money or card. Support on WhatsApp at +237 679 690 703.
  • Also: QR table ordering, branches, accounting, and an Android app with notifications.

Limits: no iPhone app yet (iPhone uses the web app), no integrated card processing, and no weighed items for supermarkets yet.

2. Loyverse: the free start

Loyverse is a popular free POS that runs on phones and tablets, with a kitchen display app and a loyalty program. Its core is free; employee management, advanced inventory and unlimited history are paid add-ons per store, in US dollars.

Offline, the app keeps selling, but its help centre says refunds are disabled and stock levels are hidden until it reconnects.

Best for: a café, kiosk or small shop with one counter and stable internet. Comparing it with LoungeOS in detail: LoungeOS vs Loyverse.

3. Odoo POS: when you want a full ERP

Odoo is an open-source business suite with a Point of Sale app among dozens of others (accounting, inventory, HR, e-commerce). Several integrators in Cameroon set it up, and partner modules add mobile money. Pricing depends on the edition, the number of users, hosting and the integrator’s work; see odoo.com/pricing and ask for a full quote.

Best for: larger businesses that want one system for everything and have a partner to configure and maintain it. If you mostly need a fast till for a bar or restaurant, see Odoo POS alternatives.

4. SambaPOS: local and customisable

SambaPOS is a long-standing Windows restaurant POS that keeps its database on a local server, so it works without internet. It is very flexible, and most venues rely on a reseller to configure it. Phones usually connect through Windows tablets or remote desktop rather than a browser.

Best for: venues with technical support that want detailed custom workflows.

5. A cash register and a notebook

Still the most common setup in small kiosks. It costs little and never needs internet. It also tells you nothing about which waiter cancelled what, which bottles went missing or who owes you money. When stock or cash gaps start to cost more than a subscription, it is time to move.

What about Square, Toast and others?

They are popular abroad and often appear in international rankings, but they are built around their own card processing and are not offered in Cameroon. If a list recommends them for Douala or Yaoundé, it was not written for Cameroon.

How to choose in one evening

  1. Write down how many tables, staff, tills and phones you have, and how often the internet cuts.
  2. Shortlist two systems and start their free trials.
  3. Run the “unplug test”: take a few orders, then switch off the internet and keep going for 20 minutes. Try a refund, a split bill, a MoMo payment with reference and a kitchen ticket.
  4. Check what the bill will be in FCFA after a year, with every add-on you need.
