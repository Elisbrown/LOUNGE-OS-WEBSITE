Epos Now alternatives: a restaurant POS without buying a hardware bundle

Epos Now is a hardware-first point of sale sold in the UK, US, Nigeria, the UAE and other markets. If you want software that runs on the computers and tablets you already have, here are the alternatives, checked in November 2026.

By LoungeOS TeamPublished November 13, 2026Updated November 13, 20263 min read

Key takeaways

  • Epos Now sells complete systems: countertop terminals, card machines, cash drawers and printers, often with promotional bundle prices, and its own Epos Now Payments.
  • Its sites cover the UK, US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, Spain, Germany, Mexico, Nigeria, the UAE and Malta.
  • LoungeOS is software only: it runs on Windows, Mac, Android and any browser, works with any receipt printer and payment provider, from US$10 a month.
  • Other alternatives: Loyverse (free core POS), SambaPOS (local Windows), Lightspeed (larger venues).
In this guide
  1. Why owners look for an Epos Now alternative
  2. The alternatives

Epos Now is a well-known name for small businesses that want a till delivered in a box. Its pricing pages lead with hardware bundles (countertop terminals, card machines, cash drawers and kitchen display systems) and its own Epos Now Payments. That suits some owners. Others already have a computer, a tablet and a receipt printer, and only want the software.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS, one of the alternatives below.

Why owners look for an Epos Now alternative

  • Hardware first: the offer is built around buying Epos Now devices, often at promotional prices with terms attached.
  • Payments tied in: card machines and Epos Now Payments are part of the package.
  • Hospitality features vary by setup: tables, kitchen screens and integrations depend on the bundle and apps you add.
