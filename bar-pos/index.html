Bar POS

A bar POS that counts every bottle and every franc

Bars lose money in small amounts: a free round, a bottle that breaks and is never recorded, a sale rung up for less. LoungeOS speeds up service at the bar and makes those small losses visible.

A bar POS system has to be fast during a rush, simple for new staff and strict about stock, because drinks are where most bars make, and lose, their margin. LoungeOS gives waiters a quick ordering screen on their phones and gives bartenders a bar display. Owners get stock tracking down to the bottle, with every loss recorded.

Faster service at the bar

  • Drinks go straight to the bar screen. Drink categories (beer, spirits, cocktails, soft drinks) route to the Bar Display System. The bartender sees the table and the drinks, with notes like “no ice” or “cold”, as soon as the waiter taps Place order.
  • Ready alerts. When the bartender marks the order ready, the waiter hears an alert.
  • Photo tiles and search. New staff find a brand in seconds, even with 80 beers on the menu.
  • Variations. Sell by the glass or the bottle, single or double, each at its own price.

Stock control that matches how bars count

Bar stock is counted in bottles, crates and litres, so LoungeOS lets you choose the unit for each item. For each product you set a minimum level that triggers a low-stock alert. That means you reorder before Friday, not during it.

Every bottle that leaves without a sale must be recorded:

  • Stock in when the supplier delivers
  • Stock out: damage, waste or loss for breakage, spills and missing bottles
  • Cost correction when the brewery changes its price

Comparing deliveries, sales and recorded losses each week shows where your shrinkage is. Industry sources suggest bars commonly lose 15–20% of their beverage inventory to over-pouring, waste and theft. Our guide on bar inventory shrinkage and pour cost explains how to measure yours.

Payments at the bar

Each bar creates its own payment methods: cash with automatic change, card, MTN MoMo, Orange Money or any other wallet, with their references, and split payments when a group pays together. When the internet is down, a Windows or Mac till keeps selling, phones keep ordering over your Wi-Fi, and everything syncs when the connection comes back. See offline POS.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use LoungeOS for a small bar with one bartender?

Yes. A laptop with the Windows or Mac app and one phone is enough. The bartender can take orders and see the bar display on the same device. The Starter plan, at US$10 a month (6,000 FCFA in Cameroon), covers 2 users on 2 devices. See pricing.

Can I track beer by the crate and sell by the bottle?

Record deliveries in the unit you count and sell (for example bottles), and use the item description to note crate size. Many bars count stock in bottles for accuracy.

Does LoungeOS support happy hour pricing?

You can create discount rules, such as a percentage off, and apply them during happy hour. Only configured discounts can be applied, and discounts can be switched off entirely.

Will it work during a power cut?

Put the till computer and Wi-Fi router on a small UPS, and phones run on battery, so service continues. A Windows or Mac till does not need internet to keep selling.
