FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how LoungeOS works, what it costs, where it runs and how your data is protected.

About LoungeOS

What is LoungeOS?

LoungeOS is point of sale (POS) and business management software for every kind of business that sells: restaurants, bars, lounges, cafés, nightclubs, hotels, retail shops, supermarkets and more. It handles tables and orders, kitchen and bar screens, your own payment methods including mobile money, receipts, inventory and suppliers, expenses and accounting, staff roles with an activity log, and reports. It runs in the web app and in the Windows, Mac and Android apps, with one account everywhere.

Who is LoungeOS for?

Any business that sells: restaurants, bars, lounges, nightclubs, hotels, cafés and fast-food outlets, retail shops and supermarkets. When you create your account, the setup wizard asks for your business type and suggests menu, inventory and expense categories to tick.

Who makes LoungeOS?

LoungeOS is made by Sigalix, a custom software company, and developed in Cameroon. It is used across Africa and internationally, with support in English and French. See About LoungeOS.

What languages does LoungeOS support?

The app is available in English and French. The website can be read in ten languages.

Platforms, offline use and hardware

Where does LoungeOS run?

In the web app at app.loungeos.app, in any modern browser, and in apps for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Android 6.0 or later. The same LoungeOS account works on all of them. See the download page.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes, for Android 6.0 or later. The Android app needs Google Chrome and an internet connection. On iPhone and iPad, open the web app in the browser. On phones, a bottom navigation bar with a “More” sheet keeps every screen in reach.

Does LoungeOS work without internet?

Yes, in the Windows and Mac apps. They keep your business’s data on the computer, keep selling without internet and sync automatically when the connection comes back. After 7 days without syncing, the till becomes read-only until it reconnects, and a warning shows during the last 2 days. Read-only means past orders, reports, printing and exports still work, but you cannot make new sales or edits. The web app and the Android app need internet. See offline POS.

What hardware do I need?

The devices you already have: a Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) computer, a Mac, an Android phone or tablet, or any device with a modern browser. LoungeOS works with touch and mouse on any screen size. Receipts print on 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers, or as PDF. See the hardware guide.

How many devices can I use?

It depends on your plan. Starter includes 2 users and 2 devices, Business 5 users and 5 devices, and Pro unlimited users and 10 or more devices. See pricing.

How do phones and tablets connect to a Windows or Mac till?

Any phone or tablet with internet can open the web app. Phones and tablets can also use the Windows or Mac app over your Wi-Fi: connect them to the same network as the computer, allow port 2304 in the computer’s firewall, then open http://<computer-IP>:2304 in the browser, for example http://192.168.1.15:2304. See connect other devices.

What happens during a power cut?

A laptop running the Windows or Mac app keeps selling on its battery, even without internet. Put a desktop computer and your Wi-Fi router on a small UPS. Phones and tablets run on battery. See running a restaurant during power and internet outages.

Account and sign-in

How do I get started?

Create your account at app.loungeos.app. Sign-up is on the sign-in page. A setup wizard then asks for your business type, name, logo, currency, taxes, payment methods and opening hours, and suggests menu, inventory and expense categories to tick, so LoungeOS is configured on day one.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. Each person has one LoungeOS account, with email and password or “Continue with Google”. The same account works in the web app and in the Windows, Mac and Android apps.

Do I need a key or code to use the Windows or Mac app?

No. Install the app, open it and sign in with your LoungeOS account. That is all. There is nothing to copy or paste.

How do my staff sign in?

The owner invites staff by email, and each staff member gets a role with its own permissions. On a shared till, staff can switch with a quick PIN. Every action goes into the activity log with a name.

How long does setup take?

Creating your account and answering the setup wizard takes a few minutes. Adding your products, prices, tables and staff takes longer, depending on how many products you sell.

Can I move LoungeOS to a new computer?

Yes. Install the app on the new computer and sign in with your LoungeOS account. Your data is kept in the cloud, so it syncs to the new computer. There is nothing to back up or restore by hand. The number of devices you can use depends on your plan.

Pricing and billing

How much does LoungeOS cost?

There are three plans: Starter at US$10 a month (6,000 FCFA), Business at US$25 a month (15,000 FCFA) and Pro at US$50 a month (30,000 FCFA). Pay yearly and get 2 months free: US$100, US$250 or US$500 a year (60,000, 150,000 or 300,000 FCFA). Visitors in Cameroon see FCFA and everyone else sees US$. See pricing.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with Business features, and there is no payment up front. At the end of the trial, choose Starter, Business or Pro to keep selling.

What is the difference between the plans?

Starter covers the point of sale, inventory, receipts and daily reports for 2 users on 2 devices. Business adds expenses, accounting, shifts and cash control (coming soon), weekly and monthly reports and email alerts (coming soon), for 5 users on 5 devices. Pro gives unlimited users, 10 or more devices, priority support and advanced admin. Several branches and payroll are coming soon on Pro.

How do I pay?

On account.loungeos.app, by Mobile Money (MTN, Orange), card or PayPal. The amount is charged in FCFA, and US$ prices are shown converted. Invoices and receipts are sent by email. For billing questions, write to billing@sigalix.com.

Does LoungeOS take a percentage of my sales?

No. You pay the plan price. Card and mobile money fees, if any, are charged by your bank or mobile money operator.

Can I change my plan?

Yes. You manage your plan, invoices and payments at account.loungeos.app, and you can upgrade from there.

Features

Does LoungeOS have a kitchen display system?

Yes. Orders appear on kitchen and bar screens, so the people preparing them see what to make. See kitchen display system.

Can LoungeOS prevent employee theft?

It removes the usual opportunities. Each staff member has their own account and a role with its own permissions, so people only do what their role allows. Every action is written to the activity log with a name. See loss prevention.

Does LoungeOS support mobile money?

Yes. Each business creates its own payment methods, such as cash, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, card or bank transfer. See mobile money payments.

Can I print receipts?

Yes, on 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers, or as PDF. Every order has a print button.

Does LoungeOS include accounting?

Yes, on the Business and Pro plans. You get expenses with categories you create (common ones are made for you), a chart of accounts, a journal and reports. See accounting.

Can I manage several branches?

Several branches are coming soon on the Pro plan. If you need something now, write to info@sigalix.com or contact us.

Security and data

Where is my data stored?

In the cloud, on the servers we use to run LoungeOS. The Windows and Mac apps also keep a copy of your business’s data on the computer, so they can keep selling without internet. Logos, images and documents you upload are stored securely in the cloud.

Is my data backed up?

Yes. All your data has a copy in the cloud, and we make a full backup every day. You do not need to make backups by hand.

What happens to my data if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. LoungeOS becomes read-only, on the web and on your tills, until you renew. You can still see past orders and reports, print and export, but you cannot make new sales or edits.

How do I keep my account secure?

Use a strong password, or sign in with Google. Do not share your own account: invite each staff member by email and give them only the role they need. Ask staff to keep their PIN private.
