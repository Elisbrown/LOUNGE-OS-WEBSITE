LoungeOS is made by [Sigalix](https://sigalix.com). You manage your plan, invoices and payments yourself at [account.loungeos.app](https://account.loungeos.app).

## Before you write to support

Many questions are answered in a few minutes in our self-help resources:

- [Knowledge base](/knowledgebase.html): step-by-step guides to every screen
- [Documentation](/documentation.html): installation, sign-in, roles and troubleshooting
- [FAQ](/faq/): plans, offline use, devices and data
- [Download page](/download/): system requirements and local network setup

When you contact support, please include your **business name**, the **email address of your LoungeOS account**, which app you use (web, Windows, Mac or Android) and its **version** (for example 2.0.0). A screenshot of any error message helps us answer faster.

## Book a demo

Want to see LoungeOS with your own products? Message us on [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/237679690703) or email [info@sigalix.com](mailto:info@sigalix.com) with your business type (restaurant, bar, hotel, shop, supermarket or other), your city and your number of staff, and we will arrange a one-to-one demo. You can also [start the free trial](https://app.loungeos.app/login) straight away: 14 days with Business features, and no payment up front.

## Where we are

LoungeOS is made by [Sigalix](https://sigalix.com) and developed in Cameroon. We support customers across Africa and internationally, in English and French.
