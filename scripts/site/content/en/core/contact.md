## Before you write to support

Many questions are answered in a few minutes in our self-help resources:

- [Knowledge base](/knowledgebase.html): step-by-step guides to every screen
- [Documentation](/documentation.html): installation, activation, roles and troubleshooting
- [FAQ](/faq/): pricing, offline use, hardware and data
- [Download page](/download/): system requirements and network setup

When you contact support, please include your **business name**, your **LoungeOS version** (for example 1.3.6) and, for activation problems, your **Machine ID**. A screenshot of any error message helps us answer faster.

## Book a demo

Want to see LoungeOS with your own menu? Email [hello@loungeos.app](mailto:hello@loungeos.app) with your venue type (restaurant, bar, lounge, hotel), city and number of staff, and we will arrange a one-to-one demo. You can also [start the free trial](https://account.loungeos.app/sign-up) straight away. It takes about five minutes to activate.

## Where we are

LoungeOS is developed in Cameroon and supports customers across Africa and internationally, in English and French.
