Expenses come with the **Business** and **Pro** plans (and the free trial). The **Accountant** role can record them.

## Record an expense

1. Press **+ New**, then **Record expense**. Or open **Accounting**, **Expenses**, then the **Record expense** tab.
2. **Date** it was paid and **Amount**.
3. **Paid to**: the supplier, landlord, technician…
4. **Category**: Rent, Utilities, Generator fuel, Repairs and maintenance, Internet and phone, Marketing, Bank and mobile money fees… Or press **New category**.
5. **Paid from**: cash, bank or mobile money. This keeps your cash and MoMo balances right in the accounts.
6. **Notes** (optional), then **Record expense**.

[[figure:loungeos-help-expense-new|The Record an expense form in LoungeOS with date, amount, paid to, category and paid from|It's in your accounts and reports as soon as you press Record expense.]]

## See your expenses

The **Expenses** tab lists every expense with its date, description, amount and status. The dashboard shows expenses for the period you pick, and the accounting overview takes them off revenue for profit and margin.

[[figure:loungeos-help-expenses|The LoungeOS expenses list with dates, descriptions and amounts|Sample expenses for Viona Lounge.]]

## Your own categories

The **Categories** tab lists the common categories, ready to use. Add your own (**Generator fuel**, **Laundry**), rename them, or archive the ones you don't need. Categories used by automatic entries are marked and stay.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I record stock purchases as expenses?

No. Record deliveries in **Stock** (Receive stock): their cost becomes the cost of what you sell when it is sold. Expenses are for everything else.

### Can I attach the receipt photo?

Use the reference and notes fields for the receipt number. Keep the paper receipts with the date written on them for your accountant.
