Prices: Starter US$10, Business US$25, Pro US$50 a month (in Cameroon 6,000, 15,000 and 30,000 FCFA). Paying for a year costs 10 months: 2 months free. Current prices are always on the [pricing page](/pricing/) and in your account.

Shifts and cash control, email alerts and payroll are coming and are not in the app yet.

## The free trial

Your 14-day trial starts when you finish the setup wizard. During the trial you have the Business features, so you can try expenses, accounting, weekly reports and QR ordering. No payment up front.

## Pay

1. Open [account.loungeos.app](https://account.loungeos.app) and sign in with the same account.
2. Choose the plan, monthly or yearly. Have a discount code? Type it there.
3. Pay on Taramoney by mobile money or card. LoungeOS unlocks within a minute, and the receipt is emailed to you.

Paying early loses nothing: the new period starts when the current one ends. Customers outside Cameroon see US$ for reference and pay the FCFA amount.

## Invoices and receipts

Every payment has an invoice and a receipt in your account, as PDF, from Sigalix (the company behind LoungeOS).

## Change plan

Upgrade any time from your account. In the app, a feature your plan does not include shows a lock and **Upgrade to unlock**, which takes you to the right plan.

## When the subscription ends

LoungeOS becomes read-only: you can see all your data and reports, but not record new sales. Pay and it opens again within a minute. Your data is never deleted.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a card?

No. MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money work, as well as cards.

### Is there a contract?

No. Pay month by month or for a year. If you stop, your data stays and you can come back.
