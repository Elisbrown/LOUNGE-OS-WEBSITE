## Add a team member

1. Open **Staff** and press **Add Staff Member**. From any page you can also press **+ New**, then **Add staff**.
2. Type their **Name**.
3. **Email**: they get an invitation and can then sign in to the web app and the phone app with their own account. The email is optional if they only use a till.
4. **Till PIN**: 4 to 6 digits they type to sign in on the till. Optional, but it makes switching people on a shared till a two-second job.
5. Pick their **Role** and, for waiters, their **Assigned Floor**.
6. Save.

[[figure:loungeos-help-staff-new|The LoungeOS add staff form with name, email, phone, till PIN, role and assigned floor|Email for their own account, PIN for the till, or both.]]

How many people you can add depends on your plan: 2 users on Starter, 5 on Business, unlimited on Pro. See [Plans and billing](/help/plans-and-billing/).

## Roles

| Role | What they do in LoungeOS |
|---|---|
| Super Admin (owner) | Everything, including billing, branches and backups |
| Manager | Daily running: staff, menu, stock, reports, approvals |
| Cashier | Takes payments, customer tab payments, sees the bar screen |
| Waiter | Takes orders at the tables of their floor |
| Bartender | The bar screen |
| Chef | The kitchen screen |
| Stock Manager | Stock items, suppliers, receiving stock (needs a plan with stock) |
| Accountant | Expenses, accounting, reports (needs a plan with expenses or accounting) |

The side menu, the **+ New** button and search (Ctrl K or ⌘K) only show what each role can open.

## Sign in on a shared till

On the till, each person taps their name and types their PIN. To hand over, sign out from the menu under your picture. A manager can reset anyone's PIN in **Staff**. Each person can set their own PIN online in **My settings**.

## Turn someone off

When someone leaves, open their row's menu in **Staff** and choose **Deactivate**. They can no longer sign in anywhere, and their past sales stay in the reports under their name.

## The activity log

Open **Staff**, then the **Activity Log** tab. It lists sign-ins, cancelled and refunded orders, price changes, drawer openings without a sale, stock adjustments, settings changes and who approved what.

## Frequently asked questions

### A waiter forgot their PIN. What do I do?

Open **Staff**, edit the person and type a new **Till PIN**. It reaches the till at the next sync, within seconds when it is online.

### Can I limit what a manager can do?

Managers can do everything except billing and owner-only actions. Give someone the Cashier role if they should take payments but not change prices or approve cancellations.
