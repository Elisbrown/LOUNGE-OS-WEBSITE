## What needs a manager

| Action | Where | Needs a manager |
|---|---|---|
| Remove items before the order is sent | Till, − or the bin | No |
| Remove items already sent, or fewer of them | Till | Yes |
| Change an item's price | Till, tap the line | Yes |
| Cancel the whole order | Till, ⋮, Cancel Order | Yes |
| Refund a paid sale | Till, Orders, Paid and cancelled | Yes |
| Open a paid order again | Till, Orders, Paid and cancelled | Yes |

Super Admins and Managers do these straight away. Anyone else sees **A manager must approve**: a manager taps their name and enters their PIN on the same screen, and the action goes through under both names. Too many wrong PINs lock approval for a few minutes.

No manager has a PIN yet? A manager sets one in **Staff** (see [Add your team](/help/add-your-team/)).

## Cancel an order

1. Open the order on the till.
2. Press **⋮** at the top of the order, then **Cancel Order**.
3. Pick or type the reason and confirm (with a manager's PIN if asked).

The items go back in stock and the kitchen and bar screens drop them.

## Refund a sale

1. On the till, press **Orders**, then **Paid and cancelled**.
2. Find the sale and press **Refund**.
3. Choose the items the customer brings back, the reason (Item returned, Wrong item, Customer not satisfied, Charged twice) and **Money given back by** (cash, MoMo…). Tick **Put the items back in stock** if they can be sold again.
4. Press **Refund (amount)**.

The money comes off that day's takings and the accounts follow. A full refund marks the sale as cancelled with the reason.

## Open a paid order again

Paid by mistake, or the table wants more after paying? In **Orders**, **Paid and cancelled**, press **Open again**. The order is open again on its table, so items can be added or changed before it is paid again.

## Where to check

- **Staff**, **Activity Log**: every cancellation, refund, reopen, price change and drawer opening without a sale, with who did it and who approved.
- The dashboard's **Needs attention**: refunds and cancelled orders of the day.
- **Reports**: **Refunds**, **Cancelled** and **Discounts** for any period.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a waiter cancel an order they just typed by mistake?

Before pressing **Place Order**, yes: remove the items or clear the sale. After it is sent, a manager approves, because the kitchen may already be cooking it.
