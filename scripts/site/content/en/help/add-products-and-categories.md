Restaurants, bars and lounges see this page as **Menu**. Shops and supermarkets see **Products**. It works the same way.

## Categories first

Open **Menu**, then the **Categories** tab. Each category says where its orders go: **Kitchen** (food) or **Bar** (drinks). That decides which screen and which ticket printer get the item.

[[figure:loungeos-help-categories|LoungeOS categories with Kitchen or Bar next to each and the number of products|Grills, Mains, Snacks and Desserts go to the kitchen; Beers, Cocktails and Soft drinks to the bar.]]

To add one, press **Add Category**, type the name, choose Kitchen or Bar and save. You can also add a category straight from the product form.

## Add a product

1. Open **Menu** (or **Products**) and press **Add Menu Item**. From any page you can also press **+ New**, then **Add product**.
2. **Photo**: press **Add a photo** to upload one, or **Find a photo** to search free photos you can use without credit, starting with the product's name.
3. **Name** and **Price**.
4. **Barcode** (optional): scan it with the scanner or type it. The same barcode can't be on two things.
5. **Quantity**: leave it empty if you don't count this product (a dish cooked to order). Type a number for things you count, such as bottles: the till then takes one off at each sale and warns you when it runs out.
6. **Category**, then **Add Menu Item**.

[[figure:loungeos-help-meal-new|The Add Menu Item form in LoungeOS with photo, name, barcode, price, quantity and category|Leave Quantity empty for dishes you don't count.]]

The product appears on the till straight away, on every till and phone, with its photo.

[[figure:loungeos-help-meals|The LoungeOS menu list with photos, categories, prices and stock|The menu list: search, filter by category or status, edit in place.]]

## Change or hide a product

- Press **Edit** on its row to change the price, photo or category. Price changes apply to new orders only.
- Set its status to inactive to hide it from the till without losing its sales history.

## Sell a stock item on the till

Things you buy and resell as they are (a crate of soft drinks, a bottle of water) can live in **Stock** and be sold from there: open the stock item and turn on selling on the till with a selling price. See [Stock and suppliers](/help/stock-and-suppliers/).

## Tips

- Short names fit the till tiles and the 58 mm receipt better: "Lager 65cl" rather than "Lager beer large bottle 65 centilitres".
- Keep drinks in drink categories even if the kitchen serves them, or they will print on the kitchen ticket.
- Photos help new staff find things fast on the till.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does my product not show on the kitchen screen?

Its category is set to Bar. Open **Menu**, **Categories**, edit the category and choose Kitchen.

### Can I import products from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Press the **⋮** button next to **Add Menu Item**, download the template, fill it in a spreadsheet and import it as CSV. Stock items and staff import the same way.
