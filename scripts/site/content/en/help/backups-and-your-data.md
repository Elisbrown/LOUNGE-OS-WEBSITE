## What protects your data

1. **The online copy.** Every till syncs to your business online every few seconds when it has internet.
2. **Daily cloud backups** of the online data, kept off the server.
3. **Backups you download** yourself, whenever you want one in your hands.

Each business is fully separate from every other business: nobody else can see your data.

## Download a backup

1. Open **Business settings**, then the **Backup & Restore** tab (owner and managers only).
2. Under **Manual Backup**, press **Download Backup Now**. Keep the file somewhere safe, such as a USB key or your email.

**Automatic Backups** makes the till save backups on its own (daily by default); **Backup History** keeps the last 5.

[[figure:loungeos-help-backup|LoungeOS Backup & Restore with system status, manual backup, restore and automatic backups|Restore replaces everything on this till: download a fresh backup first.]]

## Restore a backup

Under **Restore from Backup**, press **Upload & Restore** and choose the file. Restoring overwrites the data on this till with the backup's, so only do it when you mean to go back in time, and download a fresh backup first.

## Replace a till computer

You don't need a backup file when the till was syncing:

1. [Install LoungeOS](/help/install-loungeos/) on the new computer.
2. Sign it in to your account. It finds your business online and brings everything down.
3. Choose your till PIN. Done.

In your account at account.loungeos.app, **Devices**, sign out the old computer so it no longer counts against your device limit.

## Uninstalling

When you uninstall the Windows app, it asks whether to keep the data on the computer. Your online copy is not affected either way.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is my data deleted if I stop paying?

No. LoungeOS becomes read-only and keeps everything. Pay again and you continue where you stopped.

### Can I get all my data out?

Yes: the backup file, plus PDF, Excel and CSV exports of sales, orders, stock and accounts.
