Under the order on the till you have **Split**, **Note**, **Move** and **Guests**. More actions sit behind the **⋮** button at the top of the order.

## Split the bill

Press **Split** and choose how the table pays:

- **Equal shares**: everyone pays the same share now, each with their own payment method. Choose the number of people, then take each payment.
- **Set amounts**: each person pays the amount you type; the last one pays the rest.
- **By item**: move some items to a separate bill that is paid on its own. Send the order first, then move items. The table then has two bills, shown as **Bill 1** and **Bill 2** above the order.

[[figure:loungeos-help-pos-split|The Split the bill window in LoungeOS with Equal shares, Set amounts and By item|By item needs the order to be sent first.]]

## Two payment methods

One person paying part cash, part mobile money? That is not a split: press **Charge Order**, then **Two methods**, and enter the first amount and method; the rest goes on the second.

## Move an order to another table

Press **Move** and pick the new table. The order, its items and its status move; the old table becomes free. Kitchen and bar keep working on it without interruption.

To join two bills into one, use **⋮**, **Merge Order**.

## Notes for the kitchen and bar

Press **Note**:

- **For the whole order**: "birthday, serve the starters together".
- For one item: tap the item's line and add "no pepper" or "well done".

Notes show on the kitchen and bar screens and print on their tickets when you send the order.

## Guests

Press **Guests** and set how many people are at the table. It shows on the kitchen ticket, and reports give the average spent per guest.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I split after the bill is printed?

Yes. The bill is only a printout; the order stays open until it is paid.

### Is moving or splitting logged?

Moves, cancellations and price changes are written in the activity log with who did them.
