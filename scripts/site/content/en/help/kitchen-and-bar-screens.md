Any screen can be the kitchen or bar screen: a tablet on the wall, a laptop, or the phone app. Sign in as the chef or bartender and open **Kitchen** or **Bar** from the side menu.

## How orders arrive

When a waiter presses **Place Order**, LoungeOS looks at each item's category: kitchen categories (Grills, Mains…) go to the kitchen, bar categories (Beers, Cocktails…) go to the bar. A table that orders a grilled fish and two beers shows on both screens, each with only its own items.

[[figure:loungeos-help-kitchen|The LoungeOS kitchen display with Pending, In Progress and Ready columns and order cards|Each card shows the table, how long ago it came in, the guests and notes such as a birthday.]]

## Work through the orders

1. New orders appear in **Pending**, oldest first, with the table, the time since it came in, the number of guests and any note.
2. Press **Start Cooking** (kitchen) or **Start Preparing** (bar) when you begin. The card moves to **In Progress**.
3. Press **Mark as Ready** when it is ready. The waiter who took the order gets a notification on the phone app.

The kitchen marking food ready does not move the drinks: the bar moves its own items. The order shows as ready on the till when both are done.

[[figure:loungeos-help-bar|The LoungeOS bar display with drink orders waiting|The bar screen only shows drinks.]]

## Notes from the table

Notes typed on the till ("no pepper", "well done", "birthday: bring the cake with candles") show on the card in a box, and print on the tickets.

## Late orders

The dashboard's **Needs attention** box counts kitchen and bar tickets waiting more than 15 minutes, so the manager sees a slow kitchen before the table complains.

## Paper tickets

Prefer paper? Turn on **Print kitchen tickets** and **Print bar tickets** in **Business settings**, **Receipt**, and choose each printer. Tickets print when the order is sent, then only what changed: items added, items removed or a new note. See [Payment methods, receipts and printers](/help/payment-methods-and-receipts/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Drinks show up on the kitchen screen. Why?

Their category is set to Kitchen. Open **Menu**, **Categories**, and set it to Bar.

### Can the waiter see when food is ready?

Yes: the order shows as ready on the till, and the waiter gets a phone notification if they use the phone app.
