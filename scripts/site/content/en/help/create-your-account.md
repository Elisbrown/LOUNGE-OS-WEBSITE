You need one LoungeOS account per person. The owner creates it first, then adds the team. The account works on the web app, the Windows and Mac apps and the Android phone app.

## 1. Create your account

1. Open [app.loungeos.app](https://app.loungeos.app/login).
2. Sign up with your email and a password, or press **Continue with Google**. Sign-up is on the same page as sign-in.
3. Confirm your email if you are asked to.

Your free trial starts when you finish the setup wizard below: 14 days with the Business plan features, no payment up front. You choose a plan and pay at [account.loungeos.app](https://account.loungeos.app) when the trial ends, or earlier. See [Plans, billing and the free trial](/help/plans-and-billing/).

## 2. Answer the three questions

The first screen of the setup wizard asks three things:

1. **Business name.** It prints on receipts and reports.
2. **Country.** It sets the currency (FCFA for Cameroon, naira for Nigeria, cedi for Ghana and so on).
3. **Your mobile number.** Pick the country code first, then type the number. It is saved to your LoungeOS account so we can confirm it's you and help you get started. It is never shared.

Then tap every kind of business you run. A lounge that also serves food picks both **Lounge, bar or nightclub** and **Restaurant**. The kind of business decides which screens you start with: tables, kitchen and bar screens for venues, Products instead of Menu for shops.

[[figure:loungeos-help-setup-wizard-start|The first step of the LoungeOS setup wizard with business name, country, mobile number and business type|Viona Lounge picks Lounge and Restaurant. Press Start selling to finish now, or More settings for the rest.]]

Press **Start selling** and you are done. Or press **More settings** to go through the six other steps now.

## 3. More settings (optional, 6 short steps)

**Logo and contact.** Upload your logo (PNG or JPG; until you add one, receipts show the LoungeOS logo), and fill in the business phone, email, city, address and tax number (NIU or similar).

[[figure:loungeos-help-setup-wizard-logo|Setup wizard step 2: logo, phone, email, city, address and tax number|Everything here prints on receipts, invoices and reports.]]

**Screens.** Sales, stock, staff, reports and accounts are always on. Turn **Tables and floor plan**, **Kitchen screen**, **Bar screen** and **Events and tickets** on or off.

**Categories.** Tap to keep or remove the suggested categories for what you sell and what you keep in stock. Each selling category goes to the **Kitchen** or the **Bar**: that decides which screen and ticket printer gets the order.

[[figure:loungeos-help-setup-wizard-categories|Setup wizard step 4: suggested categories for what you sell and what you keep in stock|Kitchen or Bar next to each category decides where its orders go.]]

**Money.** Check the currency, turn tax on only if you charge it, and keep the payment methods you take: Cash, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, Card, Bank transfer, or add your own. **Ask for a reference** makes the cashier type the transaction number (useful for mobile money).

[[figure:loungeos-help-setup-wizard-money|Setup wizard step 5: currency, tax and payment methods|Turn on Ask for a reference for mobile money so every payment can be checked.]]

**Receipts and business day.** Pick **58 mm** or **80 mm** for your receipt printer, add a message for the bottom of receipts, and set when your business day ends. With 04:00, sales after midnight still count for the evening before.

**Your team.** Add the people who will use LoungeOS. You can also do this later in Staff (see [Add your team](/help/add-your-team/)).

Press **Finish**. LoungeOS says **You are all set** and offers **Make my first sale** or **Open my dashboard**.

[[figure:loungeos-help-setup-done|LoungeOS setup finished: You are all set, with Make my first sale and Open my dashboard|Your business is ready to sell.]]

## What to do next

1. [Add your products and categories](/help/add-products-and-categories/), with photos and prices.
2. [Set up tables and floors](/help/tables-and-floors/) if you serve at tables.
3. [Install LoungeOS](/help/install-loungeos/) on your till computer and your phone.
4. [Make your first sale](/help/make-a-sale/).

Every answer can be changed later in **Business settings** (the cog at the bottom of the side menu).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a licence key?

No. There are no keys or codes to buy. You sign in with your LoungeOS account and your plan follows the account.

### Can I change the business type later?

Yes. In **Business settings**, **General**, you can turn screens such as tables, kitchen and bar on or off at any time.

### Why does LoungeOS ask for my mobile number?

To confirm it's you if you lose access, and so the team can help you get started on WhatsApp or by phone. It is never shared or shown to others. On a till it also prints on receipts as the business phone until you change it.
