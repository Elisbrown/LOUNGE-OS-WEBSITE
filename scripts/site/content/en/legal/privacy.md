## Who we are

LoungeOS is made and operated by [Sigalix](https://sigalix.com), a software company in Cameroon (taxpayer number NIU P040315994194C). This policy covers this website (loungeos.app), the web app at app.loungeos.app, the Windows, Mac and Android apps, and the account site at account.loungeos.app.

There are two kinds of data in LoungeOS:

- **Your account data** (your name, email, plan, payments). Sigalix decides how this is used and is responsible for it.
- **Your business data** (products, sales, staff, customers, guests). It belongs to your business. You decide what goes in it, and we store and process it only to run LoungeOS for you.

## What we collect

- **Account:** your name, email address, mobile number and password. With **Continue with Google**, Google sends us your name and email address; we never see your Google password.
- **Business data:** your business name, logo, type, currency, taxes, payment methods and opening hours, your products, prices, orders, sales, tables, stock, suppliers, expenses, accounting entries and reports.
- **People your business records:** staff names, emails, roles and PINs, shifts, cash counts, salaries and payslips; customer names, phone numbers and tab balances; hotel guests and bookings, including the guest register details you enter; and the name, phone number and delivery address a guest gives when ordering from your QR code or online menu.
- **Uploaded files:** logos, product photos and documents you upload.
- **Billing:** your plan, invoices, receipts and payment status. Card and Mobile Money details are entered on our payment provider's page, not stored by us.
- **Devices and security:** the devices signed in to your account, the app version, sign-ins and failed sign-ins with the time, browser or device type and an approximate location worked out from the IP address (never GPS), and crash reports. We use this to count devices for your plan, keep tills in sync and spot suspicious sign-ins. Owners see it under **Security & devices**.
- **Activity log:** who did what in your business (for example a void, a refund or a price change), so owners can check their team.
- **Emails we send:** whether a LoungeOS email was opened and which links were clicked, so we know our messages arrive and are useful.
- **Support:** messages, screenshots and files you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp.
- **This website:** see [Cookies and this website](#cookies-and-this-website).

## Why we use it

- To create your account, sign you in and run LoungeOS: selling, syncing devices, printing, reports and backups.
- To bill your subscription and send invoices, receipts and renewal reminders.
- To keep LoungeOS secure: device limits, sign-in history, fraud and abuse checks, crash fixing.
- To help you: answer support requests, and send service emails such as a welcome email or one check-in after sign-up. You can unsubscribe from non-billing emails at any time with the link in each email.
- To improve LoungeOS from usage counts that do not identify your customers.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your business data or your customers' data for advertising.

## Where your data is stored

Your business data lives in our cloud database, hosted by Hostinger. Uploaded files and the daily full backup of the database are stored in Cloudflare R2. The Windows and Mac apps also keep a copy of your business data on the computer so the till keeps selling without internet. Phones, tablets and the web app keep the sales made while offline on the device until they are sent. Everything syncs to the cloud when the connection comes back.

## Providers we use

These companies process data for us, only to provide their service:

| Provider | What for |
|---|---|
| Hostinger | Hosting the websites and the cloud database |

## Support access

When you ask for help, an authorised member of the LoungeOS team can open your business to see what you see. Each opening needs a one-time code, ends when they sign out, and is logged. We use it only to help you.

## How long we keep data

We keep your account and business data while your account exists. When a trial or subscription ends, LoungeOS becomes read-only and nothing is deleted, so you can come back to it. If you ask us to delete your account or your business, we delete it from the live database, and it leaves the backups as they are replaced. We keep invoices and payment records as long as the law requires.

## Security

Each person signs in with their own account or staff PIN and sees only what their role allows. Connections to our cloud use HTTPS, every business is kept separate from the others in the database, and the till apps check that they are signed in to your account. You are responsible for strong passwords, private PINs, giving staff only the roles they need, and keeping your devices and Wi-Fi secure.

## Your rights

You can see, correct and export your business records in LoungeOS at any time. You can also ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop a use you object to. Email [info@sigalix.com](mailto:info@sigalix.com) and we answer within 30 days.

If you are a customer, guest or staff member of a business that uses LoungeOS, please contact that business first: it decides what it records about you. We will help it answer you.

These rights apply under Cameroon's personal data law and, where it applies, the European GDPR and other local laws.

## Children

LoungeOS is a tool for businesses and is not meant for children under 16.

## Cookies and this website

loungeos.app uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand which pages are visited. You can manage these cookies in the cookie banner. It also remembers your language and currency in your browser, and asks ipapi for your approximate country to show prices in FCFA or US$. Choosing a language other than English or French uses Google Translate. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy.

## Changes

We update this policy when LoungeOS changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
