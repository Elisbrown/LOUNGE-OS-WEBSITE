Clover is a payment terminal and a point of sale in one, sold by Fiserv and the banks and resellers it works with. It is a sensible choice when your bank offers it on good terms. Owners look for an alternative when Clover is not offered in their country, or when they want the POS software separate from their card processing contract.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS, one of the alternatives below.

## Where Clover falls short

- **Limited markets:** when Clover launched in Australia in 2025, Fiserv said it was then in 11 countries. Its main markets are the US, Canada, the UK, Ireland, parts of Europe and Australia.
- **Hardware and processing together:** Clover runs on Clover devices with Fiserv processing, often through a reseller contract. Changing processor can mean changing your POS.
- **Restaurant depth depends on plan and apps:** table service, kitchen display and inventory features vary by the plan and app-market add-ons you choose.
