Zoho is a natural first stop for business owners who already use Zoho Mail, Zoho Books or Zoho CRM. When they open a restaurant, a bar or a hotel outlet, they find that Zoho's point of sale was designed for shops.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS, one of the alternatives below.

## What Zoho offers for points of sale

### Zoho POS is a retail POS

[Zoho POS](https://www.zoho.com/pos/pricing.html) has a free plan, then Standard, Professional and Premium plans per location, roughly US$15 to US$75 a month depending on billing, with a 15-day trial. Its features are retail ones: inventory, barcode generation, price lists and multiple locations. In November 2026 the pricing page lists **no table management, no floor plan and no kitchen display**.

### Zoho Inventory has no till

[Zoho Inventory](https://www.zoho.com/inventory/pricing/) runs from US$29 to US$249 a month (billed yearly), priced by orders, users and locations. It is good at stock, purchase orders and multiple warehouses, but it does not take sales at a counter.

### Restaurants need a different flow

A restaurant needs orders by table, courses sent to a kitchen screen, drinks to the bar, bills split and tabs left open, and control over who can cancel what. Retail software can be bent to do some of this, but not during a busy Friday service.
