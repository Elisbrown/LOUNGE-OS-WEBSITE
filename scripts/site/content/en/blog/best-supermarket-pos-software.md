Mini-markets, grocery stores and supermarkets share the same pressures: queues at the checkout, shelves that run empty, and tills that don't balance with several cashiers on shift.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS. We say where another product fits better.

## What a supermarket needs

1. **Barcode checkout** with any USB or Bluetooth scanner.
2. **Several tills and cashiers**, each with their own account and PIN.
3. **Stock that moves with every sale**, with deliveries, losses and corrections recorded.
4. **Voids, refunds and price changes need a manager**, with an activity log.
5. **Offline selling**, because a queue does not wait for the internet.
6. **Split payments** across cash, card and mobile money.
