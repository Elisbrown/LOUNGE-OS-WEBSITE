Screws, fittings, cables, cement and paint: a hardware store's stock is huge and its margins depend on prices nobody wrote down. When the owner is away, sellers guess, and builders' credit lives in a notebook.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS. We say where another product fits better.

## What a hardware store needs

1. **Find any item fast** by barcode or by name, even items without a label.
2. **One fixed price per item** that sellers can't change without a manager.
3. **Credit for builders:** sales on account, part payments, and what each contractor owes.
4. **Stock and supplier deliveries**, with low-stock alerts.
5. **Several shops** with their own stock, seen side by side.
