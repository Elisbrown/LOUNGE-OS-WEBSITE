Square made card payments simple for small businesses, and Square for Restaurants is a capable product. Two things send owners looking for an alternative: Square is not sold in their country, or the per-device add-ons make the monthly bill much larger than the plan price.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS, one of the alternatives below. Where another product fits you better, we say so.

## Where Square falls short

### Card payments in eight countries

According to [Square's international availability page](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/4956), card acceptance with Square works in the **United States, Canada, Australia, Japan, the United Kingdom, Ireland, France and Spain**. The app downloads almost everywhere, but outside those countries you cannot take card payments with it. That rules Square out across most of Africa, the Middle East, Latin America and Asia.

### Payments and software come as one package

Square earns most of its money from card processing, so its point of sale is built around Square payments. If your customers pay in cash, by bank transfer or with mobile money, you are paying for a system designed around a payment method you barely use.

### Add-ons per device

On the [US pricing page](https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/restaurants/pricing) (November 2026), Square for Restaurants has Free, Plus and Premium plans. The Square KDS kitchen display app costs US$20 to US$30 a month **per device** depending on the plan, and Square Kiosk US$99 a month per device. A venue with two kitchen screens and a kiosk adds well over US$100 a month before payment fees.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Square available in Africa?

Not for card payments. Square's own help centre lists eight countries for card acceptance, none of them in Africa.

### Can LoungeOS take card payments?

LoungeOS records card payments made on your bank's or provider's terminal, alongside cash, transfers and mobile money. It does not process the card itself, so you keep the payment provider you already have.

### What is the cheapest Square alternative for a restaurant?

Loyverse is free for the core POS. LoungeOS starts at US$10 a month and includes kitchen and bar screens, tables and staff roles that cost extra elsewhere.
