Epos Now is a well-known name for small businesses that want a till delivered in a box. Its [pricing pages](https://www.eposnow.com/us/pricing/) lead with hardware bundles (countertop terminals, card machines, cash drawers and kitchen display systems) and its own Epos Now Payments. That suits some owners. Others already have a computer, a tablet and a receipt printer, and only want the software.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS, one of the alternatives below.

## Why owners look for an Epos Now alternative

- **Hardware first:** the offer is built around buying Epos Now devices, often at promotional prices with terms attached.
- **Payments tied in:** card machines and Epos Now Payments are part of the package.
- **Hospitality features vary by setup:** tables, kitchen screens and integrations depend on the bundle and apps you add.
