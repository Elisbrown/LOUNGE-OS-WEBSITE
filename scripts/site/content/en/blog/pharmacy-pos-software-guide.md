Many independent pharmacies still run on a sales book and a stock count once a month. The result: a customer asks for a common medicine and the box you thought you had is gone, and small, valuable items leave without anyone noticing.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS. This guide says plainly what it does today and what it doesn't.

## What a pharmacy needs

1. **Barcode sales:** scan the box or search by name.
2. **Stock counts and low-stock alerts** so best sellers never run out.
3. **Suppliers and deliveries** recorded, with the value of the stock you hold.
4. **Staff accounts and PINs:** cancels and price changes need the pharmacist in charge.
5. **Payment methods:** cash, mobile money, card, and insurance as its own method.
6. **Batch numbers and expiry dates**, with alerts before products expire.
7. **Units and packs:** sell a blister or the whole box.
