Whether you sell clothes, shoes, phones, cosmetics or accessories, the problems are the same: stock nobody can count, credit written in a notebook, and a cash box several sellers share. A good point of sale fixes all three.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS. We say where another product fits better.

## What a shop needs from its POS

1. **Barcode checkout:** scan a label or search by name or photo.
2. **Stock that moves with every sale**, with low-stock alerts and supplier records.
3. **Customer credit:** sell now, get paid later, and see what each customer owes.
4. **Cash control:** each seller signs in with a PIN, and price changes or cancels need a manager.
5. **Receipts and a cash drawer** on standard thermal printers.
6. **Works when the internet doesn't.**
