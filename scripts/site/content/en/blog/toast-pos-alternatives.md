Toast is a complete restaurant platform in the markets it serves: point of sale, kitchen displays, online ordering, payroll and lending. Restaurant owners look elsewhere for three reasons: Toast is not sold in their country, they do not want to buy Toast hardware, or they want to keep their own payment provider.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS, one of the alternatives below.

## Where Toast falls short

### A short list of countries

Toast's own 2026 announcements cover the **United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland and Australia**, and its [UK pages](https://pos.toasttab.com/uk/international-expansion) warn that not all features are available in every market. Restaurants in Africa, the Middle East, Latin America and most of Asia cannot buy it.

### Toast hardware and Toast payments

Toast is sold as a system: Toast terminals, Toast handhelds and Toast's integrated card processing. That is convenient on day one, but you cannot run it on the laptop and Android tablets you already have, and changing payment provider means changing POS.

### Built for American service

Tipping flows, US tax handling and payroll are strengths in the US and irrelevant elsewhere. What restaurants in other markets need, such as mobile money, power and internet cuts, and cash control, is not where Toast's roadmap is focused.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Toast available in Africa or Europe?

In Europe, Toast is sold in the UK and Ireland. It is not sold in Africa.

### Can I use Toast on my own iPad or Android tablet?

Toast is designed around its own hardware. LoungeOS, Loyverse and SambaPOS run on devices you already own.

### What is the best Toast alternative for a bar?

For a bar or lounge with table service, a separate bar and kitchen, and staff to control, LoungeOS. For a single counter with stable internet, Loyverse.
