Cafés, snack bars, fast-food counters and bakeries win on speed. The till has to take an order in seconds, the kitchen has to see it immediately, and the cashier has to take cash, card or mobile money without slowing the queue.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS. We say where another product fits better.

## What a counter business needs

1. **One-tap ordering** with item photos and search.
2. **Kitchen screen** so orders reach the kitchen without paper tickets.
3. **Takeaway and eat-in** on the same till.
4. **Split payments** across cash, card and mobile money.
5. **Keeps selling offline** during internet cuts.
6. **Stock** of ingredients and drinks, with alerts.
