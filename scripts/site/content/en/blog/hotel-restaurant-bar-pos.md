In many hotels, the restaurant, the bar and the pool bar each keep their own notebook. Room service trays go up without a trace, and banquets are priced on paper. The manager pieces it together at month end.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS. We say plainly what it does for hotels today.

## What a hotel's food and drink side needs

1. **Every outlet in one place:** restaurant, bars, room service, each with its own tables and staff.
2. **Kitchen and bar screens** that receive orders from every outlet.
3. **Events and banquets** planned and priced in the system.
4. **F&B stock:** drinks, food and minibar, with suppliers and alerts.
5. **Staff roles and approvals** for cancels and refunds.
6. **Rooms and charge to room**, so F&B lands on the guest's bill.
