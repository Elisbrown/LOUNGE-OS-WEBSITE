Loyverse deserves its popularity. It's free to start, runs on a phone or tablet, includes a kitchen display app and is easy for staff to learn. Thousands of small cafés, shops and bars around the world run on it.

But many restaurant and bar owners reach a point where they're paying for add-ons, working around offline gaps and still can't explain why the stock count is short. This guide helps you decide whether that's you, and what to look at next.

## What Loyverse does well

- **Free core POS** with sales, receipts, basic inventory and reports
- **Runs on phones and tablets** you already own
- **Kitchen display and customer display apps**
- **Simple to learn** and quick to set up
- **Large user community** and many integrations

For a single-counter café with one or two staff and stable internet, it's often the right answer.

## Where restaurants and bars start to struggle

### 1. Paid add-ons add up

Loyverse's paid features include **employee management** (access rights, sales by employee, timecards), **advanced inventory** (purchase orders, stock valuation) and **unlimited sales history**. Each add-on is priced per store: in November 2026 [Loyverse's pricing page](https://loyverse.com/pricing) listed unlimited sales history at US$7 a month, employee management at US$25 a month and advanced inventory at US$25 a month (or about 10 months' price when paid yearly), each with a 14-day free trial. A bar that needs all three pays about US$57 a month per store, and the bill grows with every outlet. Check the page for current amounts before you budget.

The add-ons you need most once you have a team, staff controls and stock valuation, are exactly the paid ones.

### 2. Offline limits during outages

According to [Loyverse's help centre](https://help.loyverse.com/help/offline-work-of-pos), the app keeps selling offline, but:

- **Refunds are disabled**
- **Stock levels aren't shown**, and negative-stock alerts don't work
- New customers can't be registered or edited
- **Integrated card terminals don't work** until the connection returns
- Sales don't reach the back office until the device syncs, and you can't sign out with unsynced receipts

If your internet goes down a few times a year, that's fine. If it drops several times a week, your team works around these limits constantly.

### 3. Table service and separate stations

Loyverse is built around tickets and counters. Restaurants and lounges with **many tables, several waiters, separate floors and a separate bar and kitchen** often want waiter sections, table status at a glance, automatic food/drink routing and manager-only bill splitting.

### 4. Accountability

When cash or stock doesn't add up, you need to know **who** cancelled, discounted or removed what, **when**, and **why**. That requires mandatory reasons, manager approvals and a complete activity log.

## Are you ready to switch? A quick checklist

Tick each that applies:

- ☐ You have **more than three staff** on a shift
- ☐ You have **table service** with several waiters
- ☐ Food and drinks are prepared in **different places**
- ☐ Internet outages happen **weekly or more**
- ☐ You pay for, or need, **employee management and advanced inventory**
- ☐ Your **cash or stock** has gaps you can't explain
- ☐ You want a **P&L** without rebuilding it in a spreadsheet

Three or more ticks? It's worth trialling an alternative.

## How to switch without disrupting service

1. **Export your items and stock** from Loyverse as a spreadsheet.
2. **Import stock into LoungeOS** with the CSV template, then create menu categories (food vs drink) and menu items.
3. **Set up floors, tables and staff** with their roles.
4. **Run both systems in parallel for two or three quiet services** while staff learn.
5. **Switch on a Monday or Tuesday**, not a Friday.
6. **Do a full stock count** on switch day so your new system starts from reality.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Loyverse really free?

The core POS is free. Employee management, advanced inventory and unlimited sales history are paid add-ons charged per employee or per store. Check Loyverse's pricing page for current prices.

### Does Loyverse work offline?

Partly. Sales continue on the device, but refunds are disabled, stock levels aren't shown and integrated card terminals stop working until the device reconnects, according to Loyverse's help centre.

### What's the best Loyverse alternative for a bar?

For bars with several staff, frequent outages or unexplained stock losses, an offline-first system with bottle-level inventory, a bar display and strict cancellation controls, such as LoungeOS, is a strong fit.

### Can I import my Loyverse items into LoungeOS?

Yes. Export your items from Loyverse to a spreadsheet, then use LoungeOS's CSV import template for stock items. Menu items with photos are quick to add in the menu screen.
