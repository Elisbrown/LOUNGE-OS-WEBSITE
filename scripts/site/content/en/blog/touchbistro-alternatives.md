TouchBistro built its name on fast iPad ordering for full-service restaurants. Owners look for alternatives when the monthly total with hardware and payment fees climbs, or when they want to mix Windows tills, Android phones and the laptop in the office.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS, one of the alternatives below.

## What TouchBistro costs

On [TouchBistro's pricing page](https://www.touchbistro.com/pricing/) in November 2026, the **Point of Sale** plan starts at **US$69 a month**, with hardware required and payment processing and other fees on top. The **Essentials bundle** starts at **US$119 a month** and includes restaurant hardware and integrated payments. Larger setups are quoted.
