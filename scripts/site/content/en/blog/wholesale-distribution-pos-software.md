Distributors of drinks, food, building materials or household goods share the same problems: balances that live in a notebook and the sales rep's memory, cartons that disappear between the truck and the store, and depots that only add up at month end.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS. We say where another product fits better.

## What a wholesaler needs

1. **Customer credit and balances:** sales on account, part payments, and what each customer owes.
2. **Stock in cartons and packs**, coming off with every sale, with low-stock alerts.
3. **Several depots** with their own stock and team, in one view.
4. **Payments:** cash, mobile money, bank transfers, split between methods.
5. **Expenses and accounting** for the whole business.
