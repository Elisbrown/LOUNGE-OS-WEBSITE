SambaPOS is a respected restaurant point of sale, especially where internet is unreliable. Its own site says it "doesn't rely on the internet to run", and its rules and automation can model almost any workflow. Owners start looking for an alternative when the person who set it up leaves, the server PC fails, or they want to see the day's sales from their phone.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS, one of the alternatives below.

## Where SambaPOS gets hard

### You run the server

SambaPOS runs on Windows with a local database. That machine becomes critical: if its disk fails and there is no recent backup, the history goes with it. Updates, database maintenance and backups are your job or your reseller's.

### Configuration is a skill

SambaPOS is flexible because almost everything is a rule, an action or an automation command. That flexibility means changes (a new printer, a new discount, a new tax) often need the person who built your setup.

### Prices through resellers

The [SambaPOS site](https://sambapos.com/) offers a one-month free trial of the full version, then points you to buy V5 Pro. Licence prices are not published there, so the total depends on your reseller, terminals and add-ons.
