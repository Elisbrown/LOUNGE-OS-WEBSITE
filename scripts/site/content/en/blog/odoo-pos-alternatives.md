Odoo is one of the most complete business suites you can buy, and its Point of Sale app is good. In Cameroon and across Africa, several partners sell and configure it, with modules for mobile money. So why do so many restaurant and bar owners look for an alternative after the first quote?

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS, one of the alternatives below.

## When Odoo POS is the right choice

- You want **one system for the whole company**: point of sale, accounting, purchasing, warehouse, HR, website and online shop.
- You have, or will pay, a **partner** who configures it, trains your team and maintains it.
- You need **custom workflows** that a packaged product will not offer.
- You have several kinds of business (a restaurant, a distribution company and a shop) and want them in one database.

In those cases, Odoo's breadth is worth its cost.

## Where restaurants and bars struggle

### Setup is a project

Odoo is configured, not installed. Restaurant features (floors, tables, kitchen printers or screens, preparation display) are there, but someone has to set up the modules, the chart of accounts, payment methods, printers and user rights. That is usually an integrator's work, billed by the day.

### The real price is more than the licence

The licence depends on the edition and the number of users; see [odoo.com/pricing](https://www.odoo.com/pricing). On top come hosting (Odoo's cloud, a partner server or your own), the integrator's days, modules from the app store (such as mobile money connectors) and the work of each major upgrade. Ask for a year's total in writing.

### Many screens for a simple job

A waiter needs to tap a table, add items and send. An accountant needs journals and statements. In a full ERP, both live in a large system where menus, settings and permissions take training.

### Offline is partial

Odoo's POS session can keep taking orders when the connection drops and send them when it is back, but everything else (stock, accounting, reports, other devices) depends on reaching the server. If the server is in the cloud, a long cut means a long gap.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Odoo POS free?

Odoo has a free Community edition you host yourself and paid editions with more features and hosting. For a restaurant, the larger costs are usually configuration, hosting and support.

### Does LoungeOS replace Odoo accounting?

For a restaurant, bar or shop, often yes: journals, chart of accounts, P&L, balance sheet and cash flow are built in. Payroll is not in LoungeOS yet.

### Which is better for a restaurant in Cameroon?

For a single restaurant or bar that wants to be selling this week, LoungeOS. For a group that wants one ERP across several companies, Odoo with a good local partner.
