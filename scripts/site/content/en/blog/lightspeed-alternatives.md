Lightspeed is a serious restaurant platform: floor plans, kitchen displays, online ordering, analytics and multi-location reporting. Owners look for alternatives when the monthly total climbs past what a single venue can justify, or when they want to run on hardware other than iPads.

Disclosure: we make LoungeOS, one of the alternatives below.

## What Lightspeed costs

On [Lightspeed's restaurant pricing page](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/restaurant/pricing/) in November 2026:

| Plan | Price shown |
|---|---|
| Starter | US$69 |
| Essential | US$189 |
| Premium | US$399 |
| Enterprise | Custom quote |

The Kitchen Display System is **US$30 per screen per month** on every plan, and Lightspeed notes that prices may vary with the business and industry. A restaurant with a kitchen screen and a bar screen on Essential is above US$249 a month before payment fees.

## Where Lightspeed is more than you need

- **Built for groups:** multi-location management, revenue centres for hotels and API access are excellent for chains and wasted on one venue.
- **iPad-first:** you buy into Apple hardware for the tills.
- **Integrated payments by default** on the entry plan, which ties your card fees to Lightspeed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a free alternative to Lightspeed?

Loyverse has a free core POS. LoungeOS has a 14-day free trial, then starts at US$10 a month with kitchen screens included.

### Does LoungeOS work on iPad?

LoungeOS runs in any modern browser, including Safari on iPad, and as apps for Windows, Mac and Android.

### Can I move my menu from Lightspeed to LoungeOS?

Yes. Export your items to a spreadsheet and import them with the CSV template in LoungeOS, then set each category to Kitchen or Bar.
