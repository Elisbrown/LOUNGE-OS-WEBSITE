A **retail POS** for a shop has three jobs: ring up a sale fast, keep the stock count right and show the owner what was sold and by whom. LoungeOS does all three for shops, boutiques, mini-markets, drinks depots and kiosks. When you choose *Shop or boutique*, *Supermarket or grocery*, *Hardware store* or *Wholesale* as your business type in the setup wizard, LoungeOS talks about **products** instead of a menu and turns off the restaurant parts you do not need, such as tables and the kitchen screen.

## Selling at the counter

- **Barcode scanning.** Plug a USB barcode scanner into the till and scan a product to add it to the sale. You can also type the code in the search box and press Enter. Each barcode belongs to one product, so a code cannot be given to two items by mistake.
- **Search and photos.** Find a product by name, or tap its picture on the touch screen. LoungeOS can find a free photo for a product for you, or you can upload your own.
- **Your payment methods.** Cash with the change shown, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, card, bank transfer or any method you create, and one sale can be split between two methods. See [mobile money POS](/features/mobile-money-pos/).
- **Receipts and cash drawer.** 58 mm or 80 mm thermal receipts, or a PDF. A receipt printer with a drawer port can open the cash drawer after each cash sale.

## Customer tabs: sell now, get paid later

Many shops sell on credit to regular customers. In LoungeOS you put the sale on the customer's **tab** with *Pay later*. The sale counts on the day it was made, and the *Customer tabs* page shows what each customer owes. When they pay, you record the payment against their tab, so a notebook full of names is no longer needed.

## Stock you can trust

- Every product sold comes off the stock count, and every delivery, adjustment or loss is recorded as a stock movement with a name and a time.
- **Low-stock alerts** tell you what to reorder before the shelf is empty, and products that run out are greyed out at the till.
- Keep your **suppliers** in one place and see the value of the stock you hold. More in [inventory management](/features/inventory-management/).

## Control when you are not in the shop

- Each cashier has their own account, and on a shared till they switch with a quick PIN.
- Cancels, refunds, reopened sales and price changes need a manager or a manager's PIN, and every action goes in the activity log. See [loss prevention](/features/loss-prevention/).
- The daily sales report shows the day’s sales and the money taken by each payment method. You can check it from anywhere in the [web app](https://app.loungeos.app).

## Works when the power or internet does not

The Windows and Mac apps keep selling without internet and sync when the connection comes back. A laptop till also rides out short power cuts on its battery. See [offline POS](/features/offline-pos/).

## What LoungeOS does not do yet

LoungeOS is a good fit for shops that sell items by the unit. It does not yet handle products sold by weight on a scale, or expiry dates and batch numbers. If you run a supermarket with a fresh counter or a pharmacy, [tell us](/contact/) what you need before you choose.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which barcode scanners work with LoungeOS?

Any USB or Bluetooth scanner that types the code like a keyboard, which is most scanners sold for shops. Plug it in, open the till and scan.

### Can I sell on credit to my regular customers?

Yes. Choose Pay later at checkout and pick the customer. The Customer tabs page shows what each customer owes, and you record their payments when they come in.

### How many tills can I use?

Starter covers 2 users on 2 devices, Business 5 users on 5 devices and Pro unlimited users on 10 or more devices. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with Business features. See [pricing](/pricing/).

### Does it work for a supermarket?

For a mini-market that sells packaged goods by the unit, yes. For a supermarket that needs weighed items, expiry dates or batches, not yet.

### Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Use the Android app or the [web app](https://app.loungeos.app) on any phone with internet. For the counter, a Windows or Mac till is better because it keeps selling offline.
