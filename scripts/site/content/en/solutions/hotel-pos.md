A **hotel POS** for food and beverage needs to handle several outlets with different staff and menus, while giving management one view of revenue, stock and profit. LoungeOS runs in the web app and in the Windows, Mac and Android apps, with one account for each staff member. With the Windows or Mac app on the main till, outlets keep working over the hotel's Wi-Fi when the internet fails, which matters for hotels outside major cities.

## Outlets as service areas

Create a service area for each outlet, for example *Restaurant*, *Lobby bar*, *Pool bar*, *Terrace* and *Room service*, and add its tables or stations. Assign staff to their outlet, so the pool bar waiter sees only pool bar tables.

Food from any outlet goes to the [kitchen display](/features/kitchen-display-system/), and drinks go to the bar display. One kitchen can serve several outlets without paper tickets moving between floors.

## Room service

Create a *Room service* area with one "table" per floor or per room range. Orders follow the same kitchen flow and can be paid on delivery in cash, by card or by mobile money, or put on the guest's bill with **Charge to room**. Charge to room only works for a guest who is checked in now.

## Rooms, bookings and check-out

The **Rooms** screen shows every room as free, occupied, arriving, leaving today, to clean or out of order, with a two-week booking calendar. Check-in records the guest register details (ID document, nationality, address, where the guest comes from and goes next), ready to print or download for the authorities. The guest's bill shows the room nights, anything charged from the till, deposits and payments. At check-out the nights stayed are added, the room is marked to clean, and check-out waits until the bill is paid unless a manager lets the guest leave owing.

## Room types, season prices and what a stay costs

Give each room a **type**, for example Standard or Suite, with its usual price per night. Add **seasons** with their own prices, for example the December holidays or a festival week. When you book, LoungeOS shows the price night by night and the total, and the guest is charged exactly that, even when the stay crosses two seasons. Add a meal plan, the expected arrival time and the guest's company to the booking.

## Group bookings

Book several rooms at once for a family, a team or a conference. The rooms stay linked: from one guest's bill you open the others in the group. Each room keeps its own bill, so one guest can pay alone or the organiser can pay for all.

## Services on the bill

Laundry, airport pick-up, an extra bed: keep a list of the **services** your front desk sells, with prices, and add one to a guest's bill in two taps. It appears on the bill and the invoice with the room nights and anything charged from the restaurant or bar.

## Housekeeping

The **Housekeeping** screen is made for a phone. Every morning it lists the rooms to clean: departures, stay-overs and anything the front desk asks for. The front desk or the manager shares the rooms out between housekeepers, and each housekeeper sees their own list.

- **Start** and **Done** with one tap. When a departure room is done, it goes back on sale and the front desk gets a notification that the room is ready.
- **Report a problem**, for example a leaking tap or a broken lamp. It opens a ticket for the manager. The front desk can take the room **out of order** until a date, and it leaves the booking calendar until then.
- Give housekeepers the **Housekeeper** role and they see only this screen.

## Invoices, registration cards and the daily report

From a guest's bill, download the **invoice** with nights, services, restaurant and bar charges, deposits and payments, and the **registration card** to sign at check-in. Send the **booking confirmation** by WhatsApp in one tap. The **Report** tab shows the day: arrivals, departures, guests in the house, occupancy, room revenue, average price per night and revenue per available room, with what each guest still owes. Download it as a PDF or Excel file. Every document comes in English or French.

## F&B stock and accounting

Hotels lose margin quietly in F&B: minibar restocking, banquet over-orders, spoiled produce. LoungeOS gives you:

- One [inventory](/features/inventory-management/) for all outlets, with suppliers, low-stock alerts and recorded damage, waste and loss
- [Expenses and double-entry accounting](/features/restaurant-accounting/) on the Business and Pro plans, with revenue accounts you can split by outlet type (food sales, beverage sales, event services)
- Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow by date range, exportable for the hotel's accountant

## Events, conferences and banquets

Use the **Events** module to plan conferences, weddings and banquets with dates, location and capacity. Event revenue can be tracked in its own *Event services* revenue account.

## Guests from everywhere

The setup wizard asks for the hotel's currency, for example XAF, the taxes that apply, the payment methods you accept and your opening hours. Receipts carry the hotel's name, address and logo. The interface is available in English and French.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can LoungeOS manage hotel rooms and reservations?

Yes: rooms, room types and season prices, single and group bookings, walk-ins, check-in with a guest register, guest bills with deposits and services, invoices, check-out and charge to room from the till.

### Does it include housekeeping?

Yes. Housekeepers get their list of rooms on their phone, mark each room started and done, and report problems. The front desk sees a room come back on sale the moment it is clean.

### Can each outlet have different staff?

Yes. The owner invites each staff member by email and gives them a role, and waiters are assigned to the service area of their outlet. On a shared till, staff switch with a quick PIN.

### Will it work in a hotel with poor internet?

Yes, with the Windows or Mac app. It keeps selling without internet and syncs when the connection comes back, and outlets connect to it over the hotel's Wi-Fi. After 7 days without syncing, the till becomes read-only until it reconnects, with a warning during the last 2 days.

### Which plan suits a hotel?

Hotels with several outlets usually choose Business (5 users, 5 devices, with expenses, accounting and QR ordering) or Pro (unlimited users, 10 or more devices, several branches with an all-branches overview, and priority support).
