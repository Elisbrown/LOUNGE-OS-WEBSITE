A **hardware store POS** has to handle many small items, big trade orders and a lot of credit. LoungeOS gives a hardware or building-materials store a barcode till, stock counts, supplier records, customer tabs and staff controls. When you choose *Hardware store* in the setup wizard, LoungeOS suggests categories such as Tools, Building materials, Electrical, Plumbing, Paint and Fasteners.

## At the counter

- **Scan or search.** Scan a label, or search by name for items without a barcode.
- **Payment methods.** Cash, mobile money, card, bank transfer or any method you create, and one sale can be split between two methods.
- **Receipts and PDF.** Print a 58 mm or 80 mm receipt, or give a PDF to a customer who needs one for their client.

## Credit for builders and contractors

Put a sale on a customer's tab with *Pay later*. The *Customer tabs* page shows what each one owes, and you record part payments as they come in. See [customer tabs](/features/customer-tabs/).

## Stock you can trust

Every item sold comes off the stock count. Deliveries, breakages and corrections are recorded with a name and a time. Low-stock alerts tell you what to reorder before a builder asks for it. See [inventory management](/features/inventory-management/).

## What LoungeOS does not do yet

Today each item is sold by the unit at one price. Selling the same product as a single piece or a full box, formal quotes and delivery notes, and trade price tiers are being built now and listed under *Coming next* above.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I sell items that have no barcode?

Yes. Find them by name or category on the till, or print your own barcodes and add them to the products.

### Can I give builders credit?

Yes. Use Pay later and pick the customer. Their tab shows each purchase and each payment.

### Can I make quotes and invoices?

You can give a PDF receipt today. Quotes, invoices and delivery notes are coming in the next update on the Business and Pro plans.

### Does it work without internet?

Yes, on the Windows and Mac apps. Sales are saved on the till and sync when the connection comes back.
