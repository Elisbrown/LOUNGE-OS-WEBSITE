A **lounge POS** has to do more than a bar till. It runs table service in several areas (main room, terrace, VIP), handles expensive bottle sales, supports events and keeps cash and stock under control through a long night. LoungeOS was designed for exactly this kind of venue. That's where the name comes from.

## Organise the lounge by area

In **Floor management**, create one service area for each zone, for example *Main hall*, *VIP mezzanine*, *Terrace* and *Bar counter*. Add tables to each area with their capacity, then **assign waiters to areas**. A waiter working the VIP area only sees VIP tables on their phone. You always know who is responsible for the big bottle orders.

Table colours show the whole lounge at a glance: green available, red occupied, orange reserved. Reserve VIP tables before the guests arrive.

## Bottle sales without bottle losses

Premium bottles are a lounge's biggest margin and its biggest risk. With LoungeOS:

- Every bottle is a **stock item** with a cost price and a minimum level.
- A bottle that is not sold must be recorded as **damage, waste or loss**, with a name attached.
- The bar display shows exactly which table ordered which bottle and when it was prepared.
- Removing a bottle from a sent order requires a **manager**.

At the end of the week, deliveries minus sales minus recorded losses should equal what is on the shelf. If it doesn't, you'll know which days and which shift to look at.

## Events and special nights

Lounges live on events: birthdays, DJ nights, after-work parties, private hire. LoungeOS includes an **Events** module where you create each event with dates, location and capacity. The dashboard calendar keeps the team aware of what's coming. Combine events with discount rules for promotions such as ladies' night or happy hour, applied only as you configure them.

## Late-night accountability

The riskiest hours in a lounge are the last ones, when the manager is busy and the room is dark. LoungeOS keeps control without slowing anyone down:

- **Personal accounts** for every staff member, a quick PIN on shared tills, and auto-logout after two hours of inactivity
- **Cancellation reasons** for every cancelled order
- **Manager-only** split, merge and item removal
- An **activity log** you can review the next morning: who logged in, what was cancelled, what stock was adjusted

When the drawer is short, you have facts instead of arguments. Read more in [loss prevention](/features/loss-prevention/).

## Payments the way lounge guests pay

Big groups split bills, and some pay by card, some by MTN MoMo or Orange Money, some in cash. In LoungeOS you create every [payment method](/features/mobile-money-pos/) you accept. Each payment is recorded with its reference, bills and payments can be split, and a receipt with your logo prints on your 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printer, or as PDF.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is LoungeOS only for lounges?

No. Despite the name, LoungeOS is used by restaurants, bars, nightclubs, cafés, hotels, retail shops and supermarkets too. Lounges simply need all of its features at once.

### Can I sell shisha or hookah?

Yes. Add shisha as menu items, for example by flavour or size using variations, and track related consumables such as charcoal as stock items.

### Can VIP tables have their own waiter?

Yes. Create a VIP floor, add the VIP tables to it and assign the VIP waiters to that floor.

### Does LoungeOS work if the internet cuts during a party?

Yes, with the Windows or Mac app. The till keeps selling, and phones and the bar display keep working over your Wi-Fi with no internet. Everything syncs when the connection comes back.
