A **pharmacy POS** has to be quick at the counter and exact about stock. LoungeOS gives a pharmacy or drugstore a barcode till, stock counts with low-stock alerts, supplier records, staff accounts and a daily sales report. When you choose *Pharmacy* in the setup wizard, LoungeOS suggests categories such as Medicines, Vitamins and supplements, Medical supplies, Personal care and Baby care, and hides the restaurant screens.

## At the counter

- **Barcode scanning.** Scan the box to add it to the sale, or search by name. Typing the code and pressing Enter works too.
- **Payment methods.** Cash with the change shown, mobile money, card, or any method you create, such as an insurer or a company account. One sale can be split between two methods.
- **Receipts.** 58 mm or 80 mm thermal receipts or a PDF, in English or French.

## Stock control

Every product sold comes off the stock count. Deliveries, returns to the supplier, damaged goods and corrections are recorded as stock movements with a name and a time. **Low-stock alerts** tell you what to reorder, and you see the value of the stock you hold. See [inventory management](/features/inventory-management/).

## Who did what

Each staff member has their own account and PIN. Cancels, refunds and price changes need the person in charge, and every action goes in the activity log. See [loss prevention](/features/loss-prevention/).

## What LoungeOS does not do yet

LoungeOS does not yet track **batch numbers and expiry dates**, which many pharmacies need. That is being built now and listed under *Coming next* above. LoungeOS is a point of sale and stock tool: it does not check prescriptions or drug interactions and does not replace the records your regulator asks you to keep. If expiry tracking is a must on day one, [tell us](/contact/) before you choose.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I scan the barcodes already on medicine boxes?

Yes. Any USB or Bluetooth scanner that types like a keyboard works. Scan the box once when you add the product, and every scan after that adds it to the sale.

### Does LoungeOS track expiry dates?

Not yet. Batches and expiry dates, with alerts before products expire, are coming in the next update.

### Can I record insurance or company payments?

You can create your own payment methods, for example one per insurer, so the daily report shows how much was paid each way. LoungeOS does not send claims to insurers.

### Does it work without internet?

Yes, on the Windows and Mac apps. Sales are saved on the till and sync when the connection comes back.
