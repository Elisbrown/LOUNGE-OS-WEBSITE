LoungeOS is **point of sale and management software developed in Cameroon** and used by restaurants, bars, lounges, nightclubs, cafés, hotels, shops and supermarkets across Africa. It runs in the web app and in apps for Windows, Mac and Android, with one account everywhere. The Windows and Mac apps keep selling when the internet is down, connect staff phones over Wi-Fi and sync when the connection comes back.

## Three things an African POS must do

### 1. Keep working through outages

Power cuts and internet outages are routine in many African cities. A cloud POS that depends on a connection either stops or switches to a limited offline mode. The LoungeOS Windows and Mac apps keep your business's data on the computer, so the till keeps selling without internet for up to 7 days and syncs automatically when the connection comes back. Keep the computer and router powered with a UPS, an inverter or a laptop battery. See [offline POS](/features/offline-pos/).

### 2. Handle mobile money properly

Mobile money is how many customers pay. In LoungeOS each business creates its own payment methods: **MTN Mobile Money**, **Orange Money**, Wave, M-Pesa or any other wallet, alongside cash, card and bank transfer. Each payment is recorded with its transaction reference, and one bill can be split across several methods. That reference is what lets you catch fake confirmations at closing. See [mobile money payments](/features/mobile-money-pos/).

### 3. Stop cash and stock leaks

Cash-heavy businesses lose money through deleted items, unauthorised discounts and missing bottles. LoungeOS gives each staff member their own account and a role, requires a manager for sensitive changes and a reason for every cancellation, and logs everything. See [loss prevention](/features/loss-prevention/).

## A note on e-invoicing and fiscal devices

Several African tax authorities now require electronic invoicing or certified fiscal devices for some or all businesses. Examples include Kenya's eTIMS, Rwanda's EBM and Tanzania's EFD. LoungeOS does not currently connect to these systems. If your country requires certified invoicing, check with your tax adviser how it fits alongside your POS before you subscribe. Tell us at [info@sigalix.com](mailto:info@sigalix.com) which integration you need, because country demand decides what we build next.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which African countries can use LoungeOS?

Any country. The web app runs in any modern browser, and the Windows, Mac and Android apps install anywhere. Currencies not built in can be added as custom currencies.

### Is LoungeOS available in French?

Yes. The app is available in French and English, and our French-language pages for francophone Africa start at [loungeos.app/fr](/fr/).

### Does LoungeOS work with M-Pesa, Wave or Airtel Money?

Yes. Each business creates its own payment methods, so you can add M-Pesa, Wave, Airtel Money or any other wallet your customers use, and record each payment with its reference.

### How do I pay for LoungeOS outside Cameroon?

Prices are shown in US$. Choose your plan on [account.loungeos.app](https://account.loungeos.app) and pay by Mobile Money (MTN, Orange), card or PayPal. The amount is charged in FCFA, and invoices and receipts are sent by email.
