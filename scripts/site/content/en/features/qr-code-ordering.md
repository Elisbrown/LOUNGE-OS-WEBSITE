**QR code table ordering** lets guests order from their table with their own phone, without waiting to catch a waiter's eye. In LoungeOS it is part of the Business and Pro plans, and it works with the tables, kitchen and bar screens you already use.

It is built for busy terraces, lounges on a Friday night and restaurants where the second round of drinks is lost because nobody came to the table.

## How it works for the guest

1. The guest scans the menu QR code with the phone camera. Nothing to install, no account to create.
2. Your menu opens with photos, prices in your currency and your categories, in English or French.
3. They add what they want, choose their table, add a note ("no pepper, please") and send.
4. Their phone shows **Sent to the staff**, then whether the order was taken or declined.

## How it works for your staff

Guest orders arrive on every till and in the web app under **From tables**, with a count and an alert. Waiters and cashiers with the [LoungeOS phone app](/features/mobile-app/) get a notification.

A waiter opens the order, checks it and presses **Take on till**. From there it is a normal order: it goes on the table's bill, food goes to the kitchen screen, drinks to the bar screen, stock comes off when it is paid, and manager approvals work as usual. If the kitchen is out of fish, the waiter presses **Decline** and the guest sees it straight away.

## Why staff accept each order

Fully automatic QR ordering sounds faster, but in a bar it creates new problems: a guest who left an hour ago "orders" from a photo of the code, a table orders twice by mistake, or a dish that ran out reaches the kitchen. Asking a waiter to accept the order costs one tap and keeps the floor in control. It also keeps the waiter close to the table, which is where tips come from.

## One code for the whole business

LoungeOS gives you one QR code for the business, not one per table. Guests pick their table when they order, so you print one poster design and put it everywhere: on tables, on the menu, at the bar and at the entrance. If a picture of the code ends up somewhere it shouldn't, press **New code** and the old posters stop working.

[[figure:loungeos-help-qr-poster|The LoungeOS menu QR code poster with the business logo and instructions|Print it from the app, or download PDF or PNG for a print shop.]]

## What it does not do

- **Online payment.** Guests pay your staff the usual way, in cash, mobile money or card. LoungeOS takes no commission on your sales.
- **Delivery and takeaway from home.** The menu is for guests at your tables.
- **Ordering through an offline till.** Guest orders go through LoungeOS online, so keep the till connected during service.

## Set it up in two minutes

Turn it on in **Business settings**, **General**, under **Ordering from the table (QR code)**, then press **Print menu QR code**. The step-by-step guide with screenshots is in the help centre: [QR code table ordering](/help/qr-table-ordering/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which plans include QR ordering?

Business and Pro, and the 14-day free trial. See [pricing](/pricing/).

### Do guests need to download an app?

No. The menu opens in the phone's browser after scanning the code.

### What if a dish runs out during service?

Products you count show **Sold out** on the guest menu when they reach zero. For a dish you don't count, set its quantity to 0 in **Menu** while it is unavailable, and decline any order that already includes it.
