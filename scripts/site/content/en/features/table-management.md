**Table management** is how a POS represents your dining room: which tables exist, which are free, who is serving them and what each has ordered. In LoungeOS, tables are the starting point of every dine-in order. A waiter taps a table, takes the order, and the table changes colour for everyone.

## Colour-coded table status

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Green** | Available, ready for guests |
| 🔴 **Red** | Occupied, with an active order |
| 🟠 **Orange** | Reserved, or needs cleaning |

When the bill is paid, the table returns to green automatically. Managers and cashiers see the whole room. A waiter sees the tables in their own section.

## Floors, zones and VIP areas

Lounges, hotels and large restaurants rarely have one room. In **Floor management** you create service areas such as *Main hall*, *Terrace*, *VIP mezzanine* or *Poolside bar*. You then:

1. Add tables with a name or number and a seat capacity.
2. Link each table to a floor.
3. Drag and drop to arrange the layout.
4. **Assign waiters to floors.** A waiter assigned to the terrace only sees terrace tables on their phone.

Floor assignment keeps the screen simple during a rush. It also makes responsibility clear: every table's orders belong to a known person.

## Split and merge bills

Groups change their minds. A table of eight wants four separate bills. Two couples who arrived separately decide to sit together. LoungeOS handles both, under manager control:

- **Split an order.** Select the items to move and choose a destination table. They move to a new order on that table.
- **Merge orders.** Choose a source table and a destination. The orders combine and the source table becomes available.
- **Split payments.** One bill can be paid partly in cash, partly by card and partly by mobile money.

Splitting and merging are manager-only actions. A waiter cannot quietly move items off a bill.

## Manage many tables at once

Before an event, use **Manage tables** to select several tables and apply one action: move them to another floor, change their status, or delete them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can each waiter see only their own tables?

Yes. Assign the waiter to a floor when you create their staff account, and their terminal shows only that floor's tables.

### Can I reserve a table?

Tables can be marked as reserved (orange) so staff know not to seat walk-in guests there.

### Can guests split the bill by item?

Yes. A manager splits the order by moving selected items to a new order, and each order is then paid separately, with any mix of payment methods.

### Does table management work on phones?

Yes. Waiters use the table view on any smartphone browser connected to your Wi-Fi.
