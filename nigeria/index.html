LoungeOS is point of sale and management software for Nigerian restaurants, bars, lounges, clubs, hotels and shops. It runs in the web app and in apps for Windows, Mac and Android, with one account everywhere. With the Windows or Mac app, a grid outage, a slow ISP or a congested network does not stop orders, kitchen tickets or payments: the till keeps selling, your waiters’ phones connect to it over Wi-Fi, and everything syncs when the connection comes back.

Built for how Nigeria pays

Nigerian customers pay with cash, POS card terminals, bank transfers and wallets, often several on the same bill. In LoungeOS you create a payment method for each way you accept money, and a table can split one bill across methods.

The biggest risk with transfers is the fake credit alert: a customer shows a convincing screenshot and walks out. The fix is a routine, not a gadget:

  1. The cashier records the payment in LoungeOS with the transfer reference or the payer’s name.
  2. Nobody releases the table until the money shows in the business account, not on the customer’s phone.
  3. At closing, the manager compares LoungeOS transfer totals with the bank statement.

Every payment is attached to a cashier, a table and a time, so gaps are traced in minutes. See loss prevention.

Configure LoungeOS for Nigeria

When you create your account, the setup wizard asks for your business type, name, logo, currency, taxes, payment methods and opening hours.

  • Currency: choose the naira (NGN), which is built in. Prices, receipts and reports show ₦.
  • VAT: Nigeria’s VAT rate is 7.5%. If you charge VAT, set a 7.5% tax rate as the default. You can add other lines, such as a service charge, as separate rates.
  • Receipts: your business name, address, phone and logo on every receipt, printed on 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printers or as PDF.

Generator-proof by design

When this happens What LoungeOS does
Grid goes off, generator starts The till computer on a UPS keeps running. Phones on battery stay connected
ISP or mobile data goes down The Windows or Mac till keeps selling, phones keep ordering over your Wi-Fi, and it syncs when the internet is back
Diesel runs out late at night A laptop till and router on an inverter keep service going for hours

A till can sell offline for 7 days. After 7 days without syncing, it becomes read-only until it reconnects, with a warning during the last 2 days. The web app and the Android app need internet. Read our power and internet outage checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Does LoungeOS integrate with my POS terminal (Moniepoint, OPay, bank terminals)?

LoungeOS records card and transfer payments made on your existing terminal or bank account. It does not control the terminal. You keep your current provider and fees, and LoungeOS takes no percentage.

How do I stop fake transfer alerts?

Record every transfer with its reference in LoungeOS, confirm receipt in your business account before releasing the table, and reconcile LoungeOS totals with your bank statement at closing.

Can I use it in a lounge with VIP sections?

Yes. Create service areas for VIP and regular sections and assign waiters to each. See lounge POS.

Can I pay for LoungeOS in naira?

Prices are shown in US$ and payments are charged in FCFA. You pay by Mobile Money (MTN, Orange), card or PayPal, and your bank or payment provider converts the amount, so the naira figure depends on their exchange rate. Invoices and receipts are sent by email.
