Before you write to support

Many questions are answered in a few minutes in our self-help resources:

  • Knowledge base: step-by-step guides to every screen
  • Documentation: installation, activation, roles and troubleshooting
  • FAQ: pricing, offline use, hardware and data
  • Download page: system requirements and network setup

When you contact support, please include your business name, your LoungeOS version (for example 1.3.6) and, for activation problems, your Machine ID. A screenshot of any error message helps us answer faster.

Book a demo

Want to see LoungeOS with your own menu? Email hello@loungeos.app with your venue type (restaurant, bar, lounge, hotel), city and number of staff, and we will arrange a one-to-one demo. You can also start the free trial straight away. It takes about five minutes to activate.
