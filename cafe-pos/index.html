Café & Fast Food POS

A simple, fast POS for cafés, snack bars and fast food

Counter service needs speed, not complexity. LoungeOS runs on a single laptop or tablet setup, rings up orders in seconds and tracks the cups, boxes and bottles that quietly eat your margin.

A café or fast-food POS has to ring up orders quickly, print a receipt and keep stock of high-turnover items: coffee, bread, soft drinks, takeaway boxes and cups. LoungeOS covers this on minimal hardware, with the same anti-theft and accounting tools as large restaurants.

A setup that fits a small counter

  • One laptop at the counter with the Windows or Mac app, which keeps selling without internet. A tablet with the Android app or the web app also works if you have a steady connection.
  • One tablet or phone in the kitchen shows food orders on the kitchen display.
  • One 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printer for receipts (optional). Receipts can also be saved as PDF.

That’s it. No proprietary terminal. The Starter plan covers 2 users on 2 devices, and you can move to Business (5 users, 5 devices) as you grow. See pricing.

Counter orders in seconds

Create a Counter table (or several, for queue numbers) and ring up orders from the photo grid. Variations handle sizes such as small, medium and large or single and double. Cash payments show the change due, and mobile money payments store the reference. The till works with touch or mouse.

Track packaging, not just food

Takeaway boxes, cups, lids and bags are real costs that most small outlets never count. In LoungeOS they are stock items like any other. When they run low you get an alert, and when they disappear you see it. Items like bottled drinks can be sold directly from stock and are greyed out on the POS when they run out.

Simple control for owners who aren’t always there

  • Each cashier has their own account, and on a shared till they switch with a quick PIN. Cancellations require a reason.
  • The dashboard shows today’s revenue, orders and top categories.
  • The activity log shows what happened while you were away, and you can check sales from anywhere in the web app.

Frequently asked questions

Can I run LoungeOS on just one computer?

Yes. One computer with the Windows or Mac app can be the till and the cashier station. Add phones or tablets only if you need them, within the devices included in your plan.

Is it suitable for a food truck or kiosk?

Yes. A laptop with the Windows or Mac app keeps selling without internet, and its battery covers short power cuts. With mobile data, a tablet with the Android app also works.

Can I sell items without tables?

Create one or more “Counter” or “Takeaway” tables and use them for counter service.

Can I accept mobile money at the counter?

Yes. Create payment methods for MTN MoMo, Orange Money or any other wallet you accept, record each payment with its transaction reference, or split a payment between cash and mobile money.
