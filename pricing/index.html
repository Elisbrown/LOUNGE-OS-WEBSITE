What every plan includes

Every plan gives you the same LoungeOS, with one account on the web app and the Windows, Mac and Android apps:

  • A point of sale that works with touch and mouse on any screen size, with tables and orders
  • Your own payment methods: cash, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, card, bank transfer and any other you create
  • Receipts on 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers, or as PDF, with a print button on every order
  • Inventory with stock movements, low-stock alerts and suppliers
  • Staff accounts with roles and permissions, a quick PIN for shared tills and an activity log
  • A cloud copy of all your data and a full backup every day
  • English and French

The Windows and Mac apps also keep the business’s data on the computer, keep selling without internet and sync when the connection comes back.

Frequently asked questions

Is the free trial really free?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with Business features, and there is no payment up front. Create your account at app.loungeos.app, and at the end of the trial choose Starter, Business or Pro to keep selling.

Which plan should I choose?

Starter fits 2 users on 2 devices who need the point of sale, tables, kitchen and bar screens, inventory, customer tabs, receipts and daily reports. Business adds expenses, accounting, weekly and monthly reports and QR code table ordering for 5 users on 5 devices. Pro is for larger teams and groups: unlimited users, 10 or more devices, several branches with an all-branches overview, and priority support.

Why do I see prices in FCFA or in US$?

Visitors in Cameroon see FCFA and everyone else sees US$. The plans and features are the same. Every payment is charged in FCFA, and the US$ prices are the same amounts shown converted.

How do I pay for my subscription?

Choose your plan on account.loungeos.app and pay by Mobile Money (MTN, Orange), card or PayPal. Invoices and receipts are sent by email. For billing questions, write to billing@sigalix.com.

How much do I save by paying yearly?

2 months. A yearly plan costs the same as 10 months: US$100 instead of US$120 on Starter, US$250 instead of US$300 on Business and US$500 instead of US$600 on Pro. In FCFA: 60,000, 150,000 and 300,000 FCFA a year.

What happens when my subscription ends?

LoungeOS becomes read-only, on the web and on your tills, until you renew. You can still see past orders and reports, print and export, but you cannot make new sales or edits. Nothing is ever deleted.

Does LoungeOS take a percentage of my sales?

No. You pay the plan price. Card and mobile money fees, if any, are charged by your bank or mobile money operator.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You manage your plan, invoices and payments on account.loungeos.app, and you can upgrade from there.
