A night with LoungeOS

One evening at Viona Lounge

Viona Lounge is a cozy restaurant, bar and social hangout in Tarred Malingo, Buea, and it runs on LoungeOS. Follow one evening there, from opening to closing. The figures and staff names on these screens are sample data, not Viona's own accounts. Every screen below is LoungeOS as it ships today.

4:00 pm · Before the doors open

The owner reads her week in one glance

Amina, the owner, opens the dashboard on her computer. The last seven days in four numbers: net sales, number of sales, average sale and cash taken, each compared with the week before. Below, what needs her attention: items running low, tickets waiting too long, and money still owed on customer tabs.

  • Same account in the browser and the Windows, Mac and Android apps
  • Summary PDF of the day in one tap

5:30 pm · The beer delivery arrives

Stock that matches what is on the shelf

The stock manager ticks what came in, types the quantities and costs from the delivery note, and the counts are right before service. Every sale on the till takes from them, every movement is recorded with a name, and low items are flagged before they run out.

  • Receive a whole delivery in one go
  • Suppliers, barcodes and low-stock alerts
Inventory management

8:00 pm · The tables fill up

Waiters take the order at the table

A birthday party of eight sits in VIP 2. Grace opens the table on her tablet and taps the pictures: the mixed grill platter, a bottle of champagne, three house cocktails. Every dish has a photo, so new staff find it in a second. The table plan shows which tables are free, busy or reserved.

  • Works by touch or mouse, from a phone to a large till screen
  • A photo on every product, barcode scanning for shops
Table management

8:01 pm · Kitchen and bar

No paper ticket to lose

The food appears on the kitchen screen and the drinks on the bar screen, with the waiter's notes in red: "fish well done, extra pepper on the side". The chef presses Start cooking, then Ready, and the waiter knows. If the table adds a dish, the ticket shows only what changed.

  • Kitchen and bar screens on any tablet or computer
  • Printed tickets on 58 mm or 80 mm printers if you prefer paper
Kitchen display system

9:30 pm · The terrace is full

The second round orders itself

On the terrace, table 5 wants more drinks and every waiter is busy. They scan the QR code on the table, pick two beers and a plate of fries, choose their table and send. The order pops up under From tables on the till and on Grace's phone. She checks it, presses Take on till, and it goes to the bar and the kitchen.

  • One QR code for the whole business, no app for the guest to install
  • Staff accept every order before it reaches the kitchen
QR code table ordering

10:30 pm · The bill

Cash, Mobile Money, split or on the tab

At the counter till, Carine charges table 3. Half in cash, half by MTN Mobile Money with its reference. The next table splits the bill in equal shares, and a regular puts his on his tab to pay at the end of the month. The receipt prints and the cash drawer opens.

  • Cash, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, card and your own methods, in FCFA
  • Split, move a table, pay later, 58 mm and 80 mm receipts
Mobile Money POS

11:15 pm · The internet drops

Nobody notices

The Windows and Mac apps keep the business on the computer, so the till keeps selling offline: orders, kitchen and bar screens, payments and receipts. Phones on the same Wi-Fi keep working through it. When the connection comes back, everything syncs.

Offline POS

4:00 am · The day closes

Every sale, every franc, every name

The daily report breaks the night down by hour, product, waiter and payment method, ready as PDF, Excel or CSV. Expenses go into categories, the books keep themselves, and the activity log shows who cancelled what and why. Amina checks it on her phone the next morning.

  • Expenses, chart of accounts, profit and loss
  • Roles, PINs, manager approval and an activity log
Accounting Loss prevention

Your own evening starts with three steps

  1. Create your account on app.loungeos.app with email or Google.
  2. Answer three questions: business name, country and what you sell. Logo, payment methods and receipts can wait.
  3. Add your items and team: a photo and price for each product, a role and PIN for each person.

LoungeOS is point of sale (POS) and business management software for every kind of business that sells: restaurants, bars, lounges, cafés, nightclubs, hotels, retail shops, supermarkets and more. It handles sales, tables and orders, kitchen and bar screens, payments, receipts, inventory, expenses, accounting, staff roles and reports, in English and French.

LoungeOS runs in any modern browser at app.loungeos.app, and as apps for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Android 7.0 or later. You use the same LoungeOS account on all of them. Download LoungeOS.
