Key Features

The Full Arsenal: Built for Operators Who Mean Business

You don't need a cash register. You need a command center. LoungeOS shows you every sale, every payment and every stock movement, and who made it.

Setup Wizard

Answer a few questions: business type, name, logo, currency, taxes, payment methods and opening hours. Tick the suggested menu, inventory and expense categories, and LoungeOS is configured on day one.

One Account, Google Sign-In

Each person has one LoungeOS account, with email and password or Continue with Google. The same account works on the web, Windows, Mac and Android. No keys, no codes to copy.

Offline Selling & Sync

Windows and Mac tills keep selling without internet and sync automatically when the connection comes back. Phones and tablets on the same Wi-Fi keep working through their browser.

Payments & Receipts

Create your own payment methods: cash, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, card, bank transfer and more. Print receipts on 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printers, or as PDF, from any order.

Expenses & Accounting

Record expenses in categories you create, with common ones made for you. A chart of accounts and a journal keep your books, and reports show where the money goes.

Backups & Staff Roles

Point of Sale & Ordering
  • Touch and Mouse: Works on any screen size, from a phone to a large till screen.
  • Tables and Orders: Open a table, add to the order and follow it until it is paid.
  • Custom Payment Methods: Cash, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, card, bank transfer or any method you create.
  • Receipts: 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers, or PDF, with a print button on every order.
  • Barcode Scanning: Scan a product’s barcode at the till to add it to the sale, or type the code and press Enter.
  • Split, Move and Pay Later: Split a bill by item or in equal shares, move an order to another table, or put it on a customer’s tab and take the payment later.
  • Cash Drawer: The drawer opens from the till, or after each cash sale, through a receipt printer with a drawer port.
  • Made for Phones: A bottom navigation bar with a “More” sheet keeps every screen in reach.
Kitchen & Bar Production
  • Kitchen Screens: Food orders appear on the kitchen screen, so no paper ticket gets lost.
  • Bar Screens: Drink orders appear on the bar screen.
  • Kitchen and Bar Tickets: Food goes to the kitchen and drinks to the bar. A changed order prints only what was added, removed or changed.
  • Any Screen: Use a tablet, a computer or a large screen in the kitchen and at the bar.
Inventory Management
  • Stock Movements: Every item that comes in or goes out is recorded.
  • Low-Stock Alerts: Warnings before essential items run out.
  • Suppliers: Keep your suppliers’ details in one place.
  • Several Branches: Coming soon on the Pro plan.
Security & Administration
  • Roles and Permissions: Each staff member only sees and does what their role allows.
  • Staff Invites and PIN: Invite staff by email; on a shared till they switch with a quick PIN.
  • Activity Log: Every action is recorded with a name and a time.
  • Manager Approval: Cancels, refunds, reopened orders and price changes need a manager or a manager’s PIN.
  • Shifts and Cash Control (coming soon): Open and close shifts and check the cash, on the Business and Pro plans.
  • Backups: A cloud copy of all your data and daily full backups.
Money & Reports
  • Daily Reports: See each day’s sales on every plan.
  • Weekly and Monthly Reports: On the Business and Pro plans. Email alerts are coming soon.
  • Expenses: Categories you create, with common ones made for you.
  • Accounting: A chart of accounts and a journal for your books.
  • Exports: Print and export your reports when you need them.
