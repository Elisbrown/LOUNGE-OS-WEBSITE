Features

Everything you need to run sales, stock and cash, in one system

LoungeOS replaces the cash register, the paper ticket book, the stock notebook and the spreadsheet your accountant rebuilds every month. Here is everything it does, and how each piece fits together.

LoungeOS is point of sale (POS) and business management software for every kind of business that sells: restaurants, bars, lounges, cafés, nightclubs, hotels, retail shops, supermarkets and more. It runs in the web app and in apps for Windows, Mac and Android, with one account everywhere. The Windows and Mac apps keep selling when the internet is down and sync when the connection comes back.

Where LoungeOS runs

You use the same LoungeOS account everywhere, with your email and password or Continue with Google:

  • Windows app for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit)
  • Mac app for Apple Silicon (M1 and later) and Intel Macs
  • Android app for Android 6.0 or later (it needs Google Chrome and internet)
  • Web app at app.loungeos.app in any modern browser

The Windows and Mac apps keep your business’s data on the computer too. When the internet drops, the till keeps selling, and everything syncs automatically when the connection comes back. Phones and tablets on the same Wi-Fi can use the Windows or Mac app through their browser. The web app and the Android app need internet. See the download page.

That gives you three practical benefits:

  • Service keeps going during an internet outage. On a Windows or Mac till, ordering, kitchen and bar screens, payments and receipts keep running. After 7 days without syncing, the till becomes read-only until it reconnects, and a warning shows during the last 2 days.
  • You do not buy proprietary hardware. Use the computers, phones and tablets you already have. LoungeOS works with touch and mouse on any screen size. On phones, a bottom navigation bar with a “More” sheet keeps every screen in reach. Your plan sets how many users and devices you can use: 2, 5, or 10 and more.
  • Your data is kept safe in the cloud. LoungeOS keeps a cloud copy of all your data and makes a full backup every day. Logos, images and documents you upload are stored securely in the cloud.

Point of sale and ordering

  • Fast, visual ordering. Products appear as photo tiles grouped by category, with a search bar. Out-of-stock items are greyed out automatically so staff cannot sell what you do not have.
  • Touch and mouse. The same screen works on a phone, a tablet, a laptop or a large till screen.
  • Preparation notes such as “medium-rare” or “no ice” travel with the item to the right screen.
  • Variations (single/double, small/large) with their own prices.
  • Discounts as a percentage or a fixed amount, from rules you define, so staff cannot invent their own.
  • Split payments across your payment methods on the same bill.
  • Receipts print on 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers, or as PDF, with your logo, address and thank-you message. Every order has a print button.

Tables, floors and waiter sections

Create your floors (main room, terrace, VIP mezzanine), add tables with seat counts and assign waiters to floors. Tables show their status by colour: green for available, red for occupied, orange for reserved or needing cleaning. A waiter only sees the tables on their own floor. Shops and supermarkets that do not serve at tables can skip this and sell straight from the till. Read more about table management →

Kitchen Display System (KDS) and Bar Display System (BDS)

Each menu category is marked as food or drink. When an order is placed, food items appear on the kitchen screen and drink items on the bar screen, in the same second, with an audio alert. Cooks move tickets from Pending to In progress to Ready, and the waiter is notified when the order can be picked up. Read more about the KDS →

Inventory and suppliers

Track every stock item with SKU, unit, cost, minimum and maximum levels and supplier. Record deliveries (stock in), breakage, spoilage and losses (stock out) and cost corrections. The inventory dashboard shows total stock value, low-stock warnings, out-of-stock items and a timeline of movements. Import and export with CSV. Read more about inventory →

Staff, roles and the activity log

The owner invites staff by email and gives each person a role: Manager, Accountant, Stock Manager, Chef, Waiter, Cashier or Bartender. The owner can adjust the permissions of each staff member, and each person sees only the screens their job needs. On a shared till, staff switch with a quick PIN. Removing items from a sent order, splitting and merging bills, and reopening cancelled orders need a manager. Every login, failed login, cancellation (with its reason), stock adjustment and configuration change is written to an activity log you can search and export. Read more about loss prevention →

Payments and mobile money

Each business creates its own payment methods: cash, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, card, bank transfer or any other way your customers pay. The setup wizard asks which ones you accept and creates them for you. Cash payments show the change due, and mobile money, card and transfer payments can carry the transaction reference. Groups can split one bill across several methods. Read more about payments →

Expenses, accounting and reports

Every plan includes the sales dashboard and daily reports: revenue, sales by category, staff performance and recent transactions. The Business and Pro plans add weekly and monthly reports, shifts and cash control, and email alerts. They also add the finance side: expenses with categories you create (common ones are made for you), a chart of accounts and a double-entry journal. One click on Sync Accounting Data turns paid orders and stock purchases into balanced journal entries. You get an income statement (P&L), balance sheet and cash-flow statement, exportable to PDF or CSV. Read more about accounting →

Set up for your business on day one

When you create your account, a setup wizard asks for your business type, name, logo, currency, taxes, payment methods and opening hours. It then suggests menu, inventory and expense categories to tick, so LoungeOS is configured on day one.

  • Currencies: XAF (FCFA), USD, EUR, GBP, NGN and GHS are built in, and you can add any other currency. You choose whether the symbol goes before or after the amount.
  • Taxes: define several tax rates (for example VAT at 19.25% in Cameroon or 7.5% in Nigeria) and set the default for checkout.
  • Languages: the app is available in English and French.
  • Branding: your business name, address and logo on screens and receipts.

Security, backups and support tools

LoungeOS keeps a cloud copy of all your data and makes a full backup every day, so there is nothing to back up by hand. If a computer breaks, install LoungeOS on another one and sign in: your data comes back from the cloud. Inactive sessions log out automatically after two hours. An internal ticketing system lets staff report equipment problems such as a broken printer or freezer, with priority and assignment.

Frequently asked questions

Does LoungeOS need internet to work?

It depends on the app. The Windows and Mac apps keep selling without internet and sync automatically when the connection comes back. After 7 days without syncing, the till becomes read-only until it reconnects, and a warning shows during the last 2 days. Read-only means past orders, reports, printing and exports still work, but you cannot make new sales or edits. The web app and the Android app need internet.

What devices can I use?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) computers, Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel, Android 6.0 or later phones and tablets, and any device with a modern browser through the web app. Phones and tablets on the same Wi-Fi as a Windows or Mac till can also use it through their browser. The number of users and devices depends on your plan.

Is LoungeOS only for restaurants?

No. It is used by restaurants, bars, lounges, nightclubs, snack bars, cafés, hotels, retail shops and supermarkets. Any business that sells, at tables or at a counter, can use it.

Can I try LoungeOS before paying?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with Business features, and there is no payment up front. See pricing.
