A supermarket POS has to be fast at the checkout, right about stock and strict about cash. LoungeOS does that for mini-markets, groceries, convenience stores and drinks depots. When you choose Supermarket or grocery in the setup wizard, LoungeOS suggests categories such as Groceries, Beverages, Dairy and eggs, Fruits and vegetables, Bakery, Household and Personal care, and hides the restaurant screens you do not need.

A fast checkout

  • Scan and go. Plug a barcode scanner into the till, scan each item and it lands in the basket. Typing the code in the search box and pressing Enter works too.
  • Find anything. Search by name, filter by category or tap a product’s photo on a touch screen.
  • Take the money your way. Cash with the change shown, mobile money, card or any method you create. One sale can be paid with two methods.
  • Receipts and drawer. 58 mm or 80 mm thermal receipts or a PDF, and the cash drawer opens after a cash sale.

Stock you can trust

Every product sold comes off the stock count. Deliveries from suppliers, damaged goods and corrections are recorded as stock movements with a name and a time. Low-stock alerts tell you what to reorder before the shelf is empty, and products that run out are greyed out at the till. See inventory management.

Every till accounted for

Each cashier signs in with their own account and switches on a shared till with a quick PIN. Cancels, refunds and price changes need a manager. The daily sales report shows the takings by payment method, and you can check it from anywhere in the web app or the phone app. More in loss prevention.

Several shops

Each shop is a branch with its own stock and team. The Pro plan shows all branches’ sales side by side. See multi-branch.

Frequently asked questions

Which barcode scanners work with LoungeOS?

Any USB or Bluetooth scanner that types the code like a keyboard, which is most scanners sold for shops.

Can I use several tills at the same time?

Yes. Starter covers 2 devices, Business 5 and Pro 10 or more. Every till sells into the same stock and the same reports.

Can I sell fruit and vegetables by weight?

Not yet. Selling by weight with scale barcodes is coming in the next update. Until then you can sell fixed packs, for example “Tomatoes 1 kg”.

Does it track expiry dates?

Not yet. Batches and expiry dates with alerts are coming in the next update.

Does it keep working without internet?

Yes, on the Windows and Mac apps. Sales are saved on the till and sync when the connection comes back. See offline POS.
