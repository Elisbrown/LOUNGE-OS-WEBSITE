The restaurant POS system that keeps the dining room and kitchen in sync, with or without internet

Take orders at the table on any phone, send them to the kitchen screen instantly, close bills in cash, card or mobile money, and see your food cost and profit without a spreadsheet.

A restaurant POS system records orders, sends them to the kitchen, takes payments and keeps track of what you sell. LoungeOS does all of that, plus inventory, staff permissions and accounting. It runs in the web app and in the Windows, Mac and Android apps, with one account everywhere. On a Windows or Mac till, a dropped connection does not stop service: the till keeps selling and syncs when the connection comes back.

How a service runs on LoungeOS

  1. Seat the guests. The waiter opens the table view on their phone, where green means free, and taps the table.
  2. Take the order. Dishes appear as photo tiles by category. The waiter adds notes (“no chilli”, “well done”) and places the order. The table turns red for everyone.
  3. The kitchen cooks. Food appears on the kitchen display with an alert, and drinks go to the bar screen. Cooks move tickets from Pending to In progress to Ready.
  4. The waiter serves. An alert tells the waiter the dish is ready, so plates don’t sit under the pass going cold.
  5. The guests pay. The cashier opens the table, checks the bill and records cash, card or mobile money. The bill can be split across methods. The receipt prints, and the table turns green again.
  6. The owner sees the result. Revenue, sales by category and staff performance update on the dashboard straight away.

Built for the problems restaurants actually have

Lost or misread tickets

Orders go digitally from the table to the kitchen screen. No handwriting and no lost paper.

Dishes sold that you don't have

Menu items carry an available quantity. At zero they are greyed out and cannot be ordered.

Items removed after serving

Removing items from a sent order needs a manager, and every cancellation needs a reason.

Internet outages on busy nights

On Windows and Mac, the till keeps selling without internet and syncs later. Phones keep ordering over your Wi-Fi.

Not knowing if you make money

On Business and Pro, the built-in ledger produces a profit and loss statement from your real sales and costs.

Expensive hardware

Use the computers, phones and tablets you already own, with touch or mouse. Devices are included in your plan.

Menu management that fits how restaurants work

  • Categories decide where an item is prepared: kitchen or bar.
  • Variations such as half/full portion or small/large, each with its own price.
  • Photos on every tile help new waiters find dishes fast. Images up to 5 MB are cropped to squares automatically.
  • Out-of-stock control through available quantities.
  • Discount rules for lunch specials or staff meals, applied only as you configured them.

Know your food cost and margins

Restaurants usually aim to keep food cost around 28–35% of food sales and prime cost (food plus labour) near 60–65%. You cannot manage those numbers without data. LoungeOS gives you sales by category, purchases from inventory, recorded waste and, on the Business and Pro plans, a P&L. That is enough to calculate food cost every week instead of guessing. Our guide to the food cost percentage formula shows how.

What you need to get started

  • A Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) computer or a Mac with the LoungeOS app, so the till keeps selling without internet. You can also start with the web app in any modern browser.
  • A Wi-Fi router
  • Your staff’s phones or a few tablets
  • Optional: a 58 mm or 80 mm thermal receipt printer and a tablet or screen for the kitchen

Setup takes an afternoon. Create your account at app.loungeos.app with your email and a password or Continue with Google. The setup wizard asks for your business type, name, logo, currency, taxes, payment methods and opening hours, and suggests menu, inventory and expense categories to tick. Then add your dishes, tables and staff. To use the Windows or Mac app, install it and sign in with the same account. See the download page.

Frequently asked questions

Is LoungeOS suitable for a small restaurant?

Yes. A small restaurant can run LoungeOS on one laptop and a phone. The Starter plan costs US$10 a month (6,000 FCFA in Cameroon) for 2 users on 2 devices, and every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with Business features. See pricing.

Can waiters take orders on their own phones?

Yes. Waiters can use the Android app, the web app in any phone browser, or a phone browser connected over your Wi-Fi to a Windows or Mac till. Each waiter has their own account, so every order is attributed to them. The number of devices you can use depends on your plan.

Does it support takeaway orders?

LoungeOS is built around tables. Many restaurants create a “Takeaway” or “Counter” table to process takeaway orders, and track packaging such as takeaway boxes as stock items.

Can I see which waiter sells the most?

Yes. Reports and the dashboard show sales and performance by staff member.
