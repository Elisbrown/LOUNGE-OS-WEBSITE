A wholesale POS has to keep up with large orders, many customers on credit and stock that moves by the carton. LoungeOS gives wholesalers, distributors and drinks or food depots a fast till, stock counts, supplier records, customer tabs with part payments and branches. When you choose Wholesale in the setup wizard, LoungeOS suggests categories such as Beverages, Food products, Household, Personal care and Cartons and packs.

Customer credit, done properly

Put a sale on a customer’s tab with Pay later. The sale counts the day it was made, and the Customer tabs page shows each customer’s balance. Record part payments as they come in, in cash, mobile money or transfer. See customer tabs.

Stock by the carton

Create your cartons and packs as products. Every sale comes off stock, and every delivery, breakage and correction is recorded with a name and a time. Low-stock alerts tell you what to reorder. See inventory management.

Several depots

Each depot is a branch with its own stock, team and reports. On the Pro plan you see every branch’s sales side by side. See multi-branch.

What LoungeOS does not do yet

Today a carton and a single bottle are two separate products with their own stock. Units and packs on one product, price tiers for different kinds of customers, and invoices and delivery notes are being built now and listed under Coming next above.

Frequently asked questions

Can customers pay in parts?

Yes. Record each payment against the customer’s tab; the balance updates straight away.

Can I sell the same drink by the bottle and by the crate?

Today you create two products, for example “Beer 65 cl” and “Beer crate of 12”. Linking them so one stock count covers both is coming in the next update.

Can I give different prices to different customers?

Not automatically yet. Price tiers and customer groups are coming in the next update. Today a manager can change the price on a sale with their PIN.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, on the Windows and Mac apps. Sales are saved on the till and sync when the connection comes back.
