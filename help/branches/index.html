Branches: several locations, one account

Each branch keeps its own sales, stock, staff and tills. You open it in a minute from your first one, and see every branch's sales side by side.

Several branches come with the Pro plan, with no limit on the number of branches by default. Other plans run one business. Only the owner can open or close branches.

Open a branch

  1. Online (web app or phone app), open Business settings, then the Branches tab.
  2. Press Add a branch.
  3. Type the Branch name (for example Viona Lounge Bonamoussadi), its city, address and phone.
  4. Under Copy from this branch, tick what the new branch starts with: - Menu and stock items (prices included, quantities at zero), - Floors and tables, - Settings: receipts, payment methods, taxes, currency, logo, - Team (same PINs and roles).
  5. Press Open the branch. You are now working in the new branch.

Sales, stock levels and tills start empty in the new branch.

See every branch

The Branches tab lists each branch with its city, your role there and today’s sales, plus All branches today at the top.

Switch between branches

Use the business menu at the bottom of the side menu, or press Open on a branch. Everything you see (dashboard, till, stock, reports) is then that branch’s.

Tills in a branch

A till runs one branch. To put a till in a new branch, sign the till in to your account and pick that branch when it connects. Staff copied with the team keep their PINs.

Close a branch

On the branch’s card, press the bin, type the branch name to confirm and press Close branch. Its tills stop syncing and it leaves the business menu. Nothing is erased: support can bring it back.

If you change plan

When a plan allows fewer branches than you have, the oldest stay open and the newest become Read-only: you can look at them, but nothing new can be recorded until you upgrade or close another branch.

Frequently asked questions

Do branches share stock?

No. Each branch has its own stock levels, so a count in one does not change another. The menu and prices can start as a copy.

Can a manager work in two branches?

Yes. Add them to each branch’s team; they switch with the business menu like the owner.
