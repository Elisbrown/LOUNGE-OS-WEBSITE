Set up for Cameroon in minutes

Create your account at app.loungeos.app with your email and a password or Continue with Google. The setup wizard asks for your business type, name, logo, currency, taxes, payment methods and opening hours, then suggests menu, inventory and expense categories to tick.

  • Currency. Choose XAF (FCFA). Amounts are shown in FCFA on the POS, receipts and reports.
  • VAT. Cameroon’s standard VAT is 19.25% (17.5% plus the 10% additional council tax). If your business is registered for VAT, configure a 19.25% tax rate and set it as the default for checkout. Businesses outside the VAT regime can switch tax calculation off. Check your situation with your accountant or the Direction Générale des Impôts.
  • Mobile money. Create MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money payment methods, or let the setup wizard create them. At checkout the cashier picks one and enters the reference from the confirmation SMS. At closing, compare the totals with your merchant wallet balances.
  • Accounting. On the Business and Pro plans, LoungeOS records expenses and produces a P&L, balance sheet and cash-flow statement. Your accountant can map the accounts to the SYSCOHADA chart for statutory filings.

Built for power cuts and internet outages

Outages are a fact of business life in Cameroon. Load shedding was announced for the northern regions in early 2026, and internet service often drops when the power goes. With the Windows or Mac app, this doesn’t stop service:

  1. The till computer and Wi-Fi router sit on a small inverter or UPS. A laptop has its own battery.
  2. Phones and tablets run on battery and connect to the till over your Wi-Fi, not over the internet.
  3. Orders reach the kitchen and bar, bills are paid and stock is updated, without any internet. Everything syncs automatically when the connection comes back.

A till can sell offline for 7 days. After 7 days without syncing, it becomes read-only until it reconnects, and a warning shows during the last 2 days. The web app and the Android app need internet. Read the full checklist in running a restaurant during power and internet outages.

Frequently asked questions

Is LoungeOS available in French?

Yes. The LoungeOS app is available in French and English. Our French-language page is at loungeos.app/fr/cameroun.

Can I pay with MTN MoMo or Orange Money?

Yes. Choose your plan on account.loungeos.app and pay by MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, card or PayPal. The amount is charged in FCFA, and invoices and receipts are sent by email. For billing questions, write to billing@sigalix.com. Your customers can pay you by MoMo or Orange Money too, and LoungeOS records each payment with its reference.

Do I need fibre internet to use LoungeOS?

No. The Windows and Mac apps keep selling without internet and sync automatically when the connection comes back. Any connection, including mobile data, lets the till sync. After 7 days without syncing, the till becomes read-only until it reconnects. The web app and the Android app need internet.

Can you help me set up LoungeOS in Douala or Yaoundé?

Most businesses set up LoungeOS themselves: create your account, answer the setup wizard, then install the Windows or Mac app from the download page and sign in with the same account. For help, contact support at support@loungeos.app or +237 679 690 703. For a demo, write to info@sigalix.com or call +237 689 699 688.

Is LoungeOS suitable for a snack-bar or cabaret?

Yes. Bars and cabarets benefit most from the bar display, bottle-level stock tracking and manager-only cancellations. See bar POS and lounge POS.
