A hotel POS for food and beverage needs to handle several outlets with different staff and menus, while giving management one view of revenue, stock and profit. LoungeOS runs in the web app and in the Windows, Mac and Android apps, with one account for each staff member. With the Windows or Mac app on the main till, outlets keep working over the hotel’s Wi-Fi when the internet fails, which matters for hotels outside major cities.

Outlets as service areas

Create a service area for each outlet, for example Restaurant, Lobby bar, Pool bar, Terrace and Room service, and add its tables or stations. Assign staff to their outlet, so the pool bar waiter sees only pool bar tables.

Food from any outlet goes to the kitchen display, and drinks go to the bar display. One kitchen can serve several outlets without paper tickets moving between floors.

Room service

Create a Room service area with one “table” per floor or per room range. Orders follow the same kitchen flow and can be paid on delivery in cash, by card or by mobile money. LoungeOS does not currently post charges to a hotel property management system (PMS) folio. LoungeOS is made by Sigalix, a custom software company. If you need room-charge integration, write to info@sigalix.com or contact us.

F&B stock and accounting

Hotels lose margin quietly in F&B: minibar restocking, banquet over-orders, spoiled produce. LoungeOS gives you:

  • One inventory for all outlets, with suppliers, low-stock alerts and recorded damage, waste and loss
  • Expenses and double-entry accounting on the Business and Pro plans, with revenue accounts you can split by outlet type (food sales, beverage sales, event services)
  • Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow by date range, exportable for the hotel’s accountant

Events, conferences and banquets

Use the Events module to plan conferences, weddings and banquets with dates, location and capacity. Event revenue can be tracked in its own Event services revenue account.

Guests from everywhere

The setup wizard asks for the hotel’s currency, for example XAF, the taxes that apply, the payment methods you accept and your opening hours. Receipts carry the hotel’s name, address and logo. The interface is available in English and French.

Frequently asked questions

Can LoungeOS manage hotel rooms and reservations?

No. LoungeOS is a POS and management system for food and beverage outlets. Room bookings stay in your hotel management software.

Can each outlet have different staff?

Yes. The owner invites each staff member by email and gives them a role, and waiters are assigned to the service area of their outlet. On a shared till, staff switch with a quick PIN.

Will it work in a hotel with poor internet?

Yes, with the Windows or Mac app. It keeps selling without internet and syncs when the connection comes back, and outlets connect to it over the hotel’s Wi-Fi. After 7 days without syncing, the till becomes read-only until it reconnects, with a warning during the last 2 days.

Which plan suits a hotel?

Hotels with several outlets usually choose Business (5 users, 5 devices, with expenses, accounting and QR ordering) or Pro (unlimited users, 10 or more devices, several branches with an all-branches overview, and priority support). Each outlet can be its own branch with its own menu, stock and tills. See pricing, or write to info@sigalix.com for custom needs.
